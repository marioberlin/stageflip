# Prestige Creator — cinematic title card

Fourth and final preset in the Creator Style pack v0.2.0 (closing the pack contributions opened by T-516). UNLIKE T-517/T-518/T-519 — which are creator-specific REGISTER lower-thirds keyed to a single channel's brand identity (MKBHD / Vox / LTT) — this preset is a **composition template** for ANY creator who wants a high-end editorial-magazine title-card register for their video intros / outros. The visual cluster is "Vanity Fair / The Atlantic / Apple TV+ documentary opener", NOT a specific channel's chyron. Centered full-frame, NOT lower-third — the title IS the frame, not a strip pinned to it. Slowest pacing of all four creator presets (~2× MKBHD's tempo) — prestige-cinematic-pace, not broadcast-pace.

## Visual tokens

The prestige-creator register is cinematic and locked: deep-black full-bleed canvas, centered editorial-magazine headline, UPPERCASE wide-tracked tagline above or below, slow fade pacing — the editorial canon shared across Vanity Fair feature spreads, The Atlantic deep-essay openers, and Apple TV+ documentary title cards (`The Morning Show`, `Severance`, `Pachinko`, `Slow Horses`).

- **Background**: deep black `#000000` — full-bleed cinematic canvas, NOT the near-black `#1A1A1A` broadcast-strip register (Cluster B sports / Cluster A news-ticker territory) and NOT the `LowerThird` primitive's near-black `#080f15` default. Pure black is the prestige-editorial register — Apple TV+ documentary openers and The Atlantic feature-essay heroes ship on full pure-black backdrops. Shared deep-black canvas with T-517 MKBHD-pro (`#000000`), distinguishing the prestige register from T-518 Vox-deluxe (`#FFFFFF`) and T-519 LTT-pro (`#FFFFFF`) white-canvas registers within the cluster.
- **Foreground / headline color**: `#FFFFFF` — white. Maximum-contrast white headline on the deep-black canvas; cross-register editorial canon for the prestige-magazine headline (Vanity Fair / Esquire / The Atlantic / Apple TV+ documentary openers all run white-on-black for the title slot).
- **Highlight color**: `#FFFFFF` — white. The `titleSequence` primitive's `highlightColor` slot is consumed by `letterform-assemble` / `palette-jump-cut` styles for emphasis tokens; in the `plate-and-credits` style the slot is set but not exercised by the v1 render path. Setting it to white preserves the editorial register if the binding-wire step later flips to a style that consumes it.
- **Layout**: centered full-frame title — `position: { x: 640, y: 360, width: 1024, alignment: 'center' }` at 1280×720 (the parity-CLI's fixed `DEFAULT_COMPOSITION`). The position is the **center** of the canvas; the `width: 1024` text box allows the headline to fill ~80 % of the frame's horizontal extent for editorial-spread headline density. NOT lower-third — the title IS the frame, framed by black margins on all sides. Distinct from MKBHD-pro / Vox-deluxe / LTT-pro which all anchor bottom-left as cinematic-third lower-third strips.
- **Style bundle**: `'plate-and-credits'` — the primitive's two-card register that pairs a `titlePlate` shot (the headline) with a `creditsBlock` shot (the UPPERCASE wide-tracked tagline). The `'letterform-assemble'` bundle is for letterform-as-environment cluster-D titles (Stranger Things-Benguiat); `'palette-jump-cut'` is for hard-cut color-panel registers (got-trajan / squid-game); `'photographic-overlay'` is for typography-only-over-photography registers. `'plate-and-credits'` IS the prestige-editorial-magazine title-card register — the headline plate paired with the credits-block tagline matches the Vanity Fair / Apple TV+ documentary opener canon directly.
- **Shots**: two-shot sequence in declaration order:
  1. **`titlePlate` shot** (startMs 0, endMs 5800) — the headline text rendered centered against the deep-black canvas; entrance `'fade'` over 800 ms EASE_OUT_QUART, mid-hold ~4400 ms, exit synchronized with the `creditsBlock` shot via shared transitionOut `'fade'`. Headline `'The State of AI 2026'` exercises the Mixed Case editorial register at 60–72 px / fontWeight 700.
  2. **`creditsBlock` shot** (startMs 800, endMs 6400) — the UPPERCASE wide-tracked tagline rendered as a single-line credits-block entry under the headline; entrance `'fade'` over 800 ms EASE_OUT_QUART (synchronized 800 ms after the headline plate), mid-hold ~4800 ms, exit `'fade'` over 600 ms EASE_IN_QUART. Tagline `'A YEAR-END REVIEW'` exercises the UPPERCASE wide-tracked register at 24 px / fontWeight 500.
- **No optical-grain, no light-leaks, no atmospheric overlays** (cluster-norm with the prestige-editorial register; deliberate divergence from the cluster-D atmospheric stack used by stranger-things-benguiat T-348 / squid-game-geometric T-350 etc.). The editorial-magazine canon is clean type on a clean canvas — no analog-film grain, no warm light-leak, no dust-particle drift. Pure typography against pure black IS the register. Adding atmospheric overlays would migrate the visual onto the prestige-TV cluster (Cluster D); the creator-economy cluster-F prestige register intentionally stays clean.

The centered-full-frame layout IS the message — at the parity-fixture reference frame the eye locks onto the headline at full opacity at the steady-state mid-hold, framed entirely by the deep-black canvas with the UPPERCASE tagline beneath the headline plate. The slow editorial pacing (800 ms fade-in → 5 s mid-hold → 600 ms fade-out, total ~6.4 s) is the post-rebrand Apple TV+ documentary-opener canonical UX gesture for prestige-cinematic title cards.

## Typography

- **`preferredFont: GT Sectra`** (`proprietary-byo` — Grilli Type's editorial-magazine display serif used across Vanity Fair feature spreads, The Atlantic deep-essay openers, and the bespoke Apple TV+ documentary opener canon). The transitional / didone hybrid construction with its sharp wedge-serif terminals and high-contrast modulation IS the editorial-magazine prestige register. Cluster F IS in `TYPE_DESIGN_REQUIRED_CLUSTERS` (verified via T-323 / T-325 spec drafting precedent and continuous across T-517 / T-518 / T-519 sibling presets), so `signOff.typeDesign` MUST be `'pending-cluster-batch'` or `'signed:YYYY-MM-DD'`, NOT `'na'`. T-520 holds at `pending-cluster-batch`; the cluster-F type-design batch review (cluster-composer task) flips this field across the four Creator Style presets in the downstream PR.
- **`fallbackFont: Cormorant Garamond 700`** (`ofl` — SIL Open Font License 1.1 via Google Fonts). Cormorant Garamond is the OFL transitional-serif-revival closest to GT Sectra in construction — sharp wedge serifs, high contrast modulation, narrow proportions — sufficient to carry the editorial-magazine register if the bespoke GT Sectra license is not BYO-wired at deploy time. Same OFL family used as the cluster-D stranger-things-benguiat fallback (T-348) and got-trajan-clockwork fallback (T-349) — the canonical OFL transitional / didone-revival fallback for prestige-typography registers. Type-design consultant approval on the GT Sectra → Cormorant Garamond fallback path is pending the cluster-F type-design batch review per ADR-004 §D4.
- **Headline (`titlePlate.content.text`)**: Mixed Case at the snapshot string level (`'The State of AI 2026'`). Rendered via `font: { family, weight: 700, size: 60, letterSpacing: -0.01 }` at the schema-prop level — the `titleSequence` primitive's `font` prop is wired into the render output (unlike T-183's `LowerThird` `font` prop, which exists in the schema but not in the rendered CSS; see T-517 trade-off for the `LowerThird` divergence). Headline size 60 px is mid-editorial register; production opening cards on 4K canvas push to 72–96 px, but at the parity-fixture 1280×720 envelope 60 px reads as the editorial spread headline at the canonical reference frame. Mixed Case (NOT UPPERCASE) IS the editorial-magazine prestige register — Vanity Fair feature heads, The Atlantic deep-essay openers, Apple TV+ documentary title plates all run Mixed Case headline bodies. UPPERCASE headlines would migrate the visual onto Cluster A broadcast-news (Sky News / ITV / RAI register) or American-tabloid (CNN-breaking) territory entirely.
- **Tagline (`creditsBlock.content.lines[0]`)**: UPPERCASE applied at the snapshot string level (`'A YEAR-END REVIEW'`). Rendered via the primitive's per-style render path at fontSize 24 px / fontWeight 500 / letterSpacing 0.08em (wide-tracked editorial register). UPPERCASE wide-tracked tagline is the editorial-magazine sub-headline canon — Vanity Fair pull-quote captions, The Atlantic department labels, Apple TV+ documentary genre tags all run UPPERCASE wide-tracked. The wide tracking (~0.08em) is the editorial signature; tight or normal tracking would read as a broadcast-news subtitle.
- **Casing transform**: `'as-is'` (default). The casing is applied at the snapshot-string level (Mixed Case headline, UPPERCASE tagline) so the primitive's casing-transform layer is a no-op. Setting `casing: 'uppercase'` would force the headline to ALL CAPS and migrate the visual off the editorial-magazine register; leaving it `'as-is'` preserves the Mixed-Case-headline + UPPERCASE-tagline split.
- **No italic, no underline, no strikethrough.** Editorial-magazine prestige typography never uses them in title-card slots.

## Animation

- **Steady-state register only at the reference frame** per the inherited posture from cluster-D titleSequence consumers (T-348 / T-349 / T-350 / T-351 / T-352 / T-353). The `'plate-and-credits'` style branch of the primitive renders the active shot per the dispatch-by-shot-kind path — at the reference frame (mid mid-hold) both the headline plate and the credits-block tagline render at full opacity at their final positions with no entrance / exit animation active.
- **Choreography target (spec)** — slowest pacing of all four Creator Style presets, ~2× MKBHD's tempo:
  - Fade-in 800 ms EASE_OUT_QUART (primitive default `entrance: 'fade'`; 12-frame settle on top of the fade)
  - Mid-hold ~5000 ms (composition-driven; the parity-fixture composition pins `durationInFrames` such that mid-hold falls in the middle window)
  - Fade-out 600 ms EASE_IN_QUART (transitionOut `'fade'` on both shots, synchronized 600 ms before each shot's `endMs`)
  - Total per title-card cycle: ~6.4 s — slowest of the four creator presets (T-519 LTT-pro: 300 ms / 2500 ms / 250 ms = ~3.05 s; T-517 MKBHD-pro / T-518 Vox-deluxe: 450 ms / 3000 ms / 350 ms = ~3.8 s)
- **Mid-segment steady-state at frame 120** (cluster-D titleSequence cluster-norm per T-348 / T-350 etc.; the parity-CLI's fixed 150-frame composition window means frame 120 = 4000 ms @ 30 fps falls cleanly in the mid mid-hold window). At frame 120 the render captures both shots at full opacity with their final positions settled — `pairIdx` is N/A here (cluster-D titleSequence dispatches by shot.kind for `'plate-and-credits'`, not by per-pair window), the active shot at 4000 ms is the credits-block (startMs 800, endMs 6400), with the title plate also active (startMs 0, endMs 5800) — both shots overlap fully at frame 120.
- **No state-transition animation in v1.** State transitions (per-letter staggered entry from `'letterform-assemble'`, per-shot color-jump from `'palette-jump-cut'`, photographic overlay tinting from `'photographic-overlay'`) belong to other style bundles and other clusters; the prestige-creator register is the clean steady-state register.

## Rules

- **Bound primitive**: `titleSequence` from `@stageflip/runtimes-frame-runtime-bridge` (`packages/runtimes/frame-runtime-bridge/src/clips/title-sequence.tsx`, exported as `titleSequenceClip`; primitive `kind: 'titleSequence'`). T-520 wires the binding via `PRESET_ID_BINDINGS['prestige-creator']` per Pattern C — the `'titleSequence'` arm in `DEFAULT_CLIP_KIND_RESOLVER` stays UNCHANGED at the cluster-D squid-game-geometric clipKind-default (T-350; first preset for the clipKind takes the default slot). All other cluster-D consumers (stranger-things-benguiat T-348, got-trajan-clockwork T-349, true-detective-double-exposure T-351, succession-home-video T-352, severance-surreal-3d T-353) bind via the same `PRESET_ID_BINDINGS` override path. **First cluster-F `titleSequence` consumer**: T-520 `prestige-creator` (this preset) — the cross-cluster register reuse establishes the cluster-F creator-economy use of the cluster-D prestige-TV title compositor primitive.
- **Cross-cluster register reuse: cluster-F preset binding a cluster-D primitive.** The prestige-creator register's editorial-magazine canon is materially closer to the cluster-D prestige-TV title-card register than to the cluster-F creator-economy lower-third register. Reusing the cluster-D `titleSequence` primitive (T-321) directly — rather than introducing a new `fullscreenTitle` cluster-F primitive — keeps the implementation surface small AND respects the structural-extension rule (§13 of CLAUDE.md): no new clipKind, no new degree-of-freedom on the document or binding model, just a new cluster-F preset binding the existing cluster-D primitive. The visual register difference (editorial-magazine title-card vs prestige-TV opener) is captured in the snapshot constants (`PRESTIGE_CREATOR_PROPS`) — same primitive, different props.
- **Editorial-magazine canon (Vanity Fair / The Atlantic / Apple TV+ documentary openers).** The register is editorial-magazine PRESTIGE, NOT prestige-TV (cluster-D), NOT broadcast-news (cluster-A), NOT broadcast-sports (cluster-B), NOT energetic-broadcast (T-519 LTT-pro), NOT cinematic-tech-reviewer (T-517 MKBHD-pro). Choose prestige-creator for the cinematic title-card opener register specifically — feature-essay video openers, documentary intros, year-end-review title cards, deep-essay slide intros.
- **Centered full-frame layout, NOT lower-third.** The title IS the frame, framed by deep-black margins on all sides. Pulling the layout to `alignment: 'left'` or anchoring to a lower-third strip migrates the visual off the editorial-magazine register and onto the cluster-F lower-third register (T-517 / T-518 / T-519 territory).
- **Slowest pacing of all four creator presets.** 800 ms in, ~5 s mid-hold, 600 ms out, total ~6.4 s. Speeding the pacing to 450 / 3000 / 350 ms migrates the visual onto T-517 MKBHD-pro / T-518 Vox-deluxe cinematic-third pacing; speeding further to 300 / 2500 / 250 ms migrates onto T-519 LTT-pro broadcast-pace pacing. The slow editorial-cinematic pace IS the prestige-creator signature.
- **Mixed Case headline + UPPERCASE wide-tracked tagline — NOT both Mixed Case or both UPPERCASE.** Both Mixed Case (Vox-deluxe register T-518) migrates onto magazine-explainer territory; both UPPERCASE (broadcast-news cluster A) migrates onto Sky News / ITV / RAI register territory. The split casing IS the editorial-magazine prestige typographic signature.
- **No atmospheric overlays.** No optical grain, no light-leak, no dust-particle drift. The clean editorial canvas IS the register; adding atmospheric overlays migrates the visual onto cluster-D prestige-TV title territory (stranger-things-benguiat T-348 / true-detective-double-exposure T-351 etc.).
- **Designed for prestige editorial-magazine cinematic openers across deep-essay video content, documentary intros, year-end-review title cards** — same legibility requirements as the cluster-A news-broadcaster register (headline ≥ 36 pt; primitive's 60 px far exceeds; tagline ≥ 18 pt; primitive's 24 px exceeds).
- **Reference frame for parity is mid-segment (frame 120)** per cluster-D titleSequence cluster-norm — single canonical variant. The `'plate-and-credits'` style is steady-state per active-shot window; any frame in `[1000, 5800)` ms produces a render with both the headline plate and the credits-block tagline at full opacity at their final positions.

## Trade-offs (v1 cosmetic divergences from the compass register)

- **Rendered family is `Cormorant Garamond 700` (OFL fallback), not the bespoke `GT Sectra` (proprietary BYO).** The `titleSequence` primitive's `font.family` prop IS wired into the rendered output (unlike T-183's `LowerThird` `font.family` divergence), so the rendered family follows the binding's snapshot `font.family` value verbatim. The binding-wire step (cluster-F composer task) sets `font.family: 'Cormorant Garamond'` for the parity render; flipping to `'GT Sectra'` requires the bespoke font to be BYO-wired at deploy time (license `proprietary-byo`) and would be the production-fidelity preference. v1 ships the OFL fallback verbatim — the editorial-magazine register reads correctly at Cormorant Garamond's transitional-serif construction.
- **Headline size 60 px, not the production 72–96 px range.** Production opening cards on 4K canvas push headline size to 72–96 px range; at the parity-fixture 1280×720 envelope 60 px reads as the editorial spread headline at the canonical reference frame. Scaling up to 72 px would push the headline closer to the canvas edges at 1280 width; staying at 60 px preserves the editorial margin discipline. Production deployments with a 1920×1080 or 4K canvas can scale up via the binding override — the snapshot ships 60 px for the parity-fixture register.
- **Mid-hold duration is composition-driven, not primitive-pinned.** The spec calls for a ~5 s mid-hold between the 800 ms entrance and 600 ms exit. The `titleSequence` primitive's shot dispatch is keyed by `startMs / endMs` per shot, so the mid-hold falls out of the difference (`endMs - startMs - entranceMs - exitMs`); the parity-fixture composition pins `durationInFrames: 150` (= 5000 ms @ 30 fps) by default. The 5 s mid-hold target falls out naturally from the composition's clip-envelope duration and the per-shot startMs / endMs pinning, not from a primitive-level prop.
- **`'plate-and-credits'` style overlap window pins both shots active at the reference frame.** The headline plate (startMs 0, endMs 5800) and the credits-block tagline (startMs 800, endMs 6400) overlap by 5000 ms. At the reference frame 120 (= 4000 ms @ 30 fps), both shots are active simultaneously. The primitive's `'plate-and-credits'` dispatch renders BOTH active shots layered (the title plate above, the credits block beneath); the v1 parity render captures this two-shot overlap as the steady-state register. The Vanity Fair / Apple TV+ documentary canon shows the headline and the tagline simultaneously; the overlap is the canonical layout, not a divergence.
- **No cluster-F `fullscreenTitle` primitive carved out.** The spec note "use existing `fullscreenTitle` clipKind if available; check cluster-F or cinematic title clip kinds" was investigated: no `fullscreenTitle` primitive exists in cluster-F. The cluster-D `titleSequence` primitive (T-321) is the closest existing cinematic-title clipKind — reused here cross-cluster. Carving out a dedicated cluster-F `fullscreenTitle` primitive (specialized for the editorial-magazine register without the cluster-D atmospheric-overlay companion clips) is a candidate **`T-520a`-family follow-up** under the cluster-F compose-tools label (T-368). The cross-cluster register reuse approach is the conservative v1 — no structural extension to the document or binding model, no new clipKind, just a new cluster-F preset binding the existing cluster-D primitive.
- **No atmospheric companion clips.** Cluster-D titleSequence consumers (stranger-things-benguiat T-348, got-trajan-clockwork T-349, etc.) compose the titleSequence primitive with grain / light-leak / particles / photographic-overlay companion clips for the cluster-D prestige-TV register's analog-film aesthetic. The prestige-creator register intentionally OMITS these — the editorial-magazine canon is clean type on a clean canvas. The binding-wire step composes the titleSequence solo (no `overlays?` array), distinguishing the cluster-F prestige-creator register from the cluster-D prestige-TV registers structurally as well as visually.

## Out of scope

Candidate sister preset `prestige-creator-letterform` IF Reviewer scrutiny demands a per-letter register; not v1.
- Photographic-overlay register (the `'photographic-overlay'` style bundle's typography-only-over-photography render path) — primitive-level style-bundle selection; the prestige-creator register on a black canvas is the editorial-magazine register. Photographic-overlay over deep-essay B-roll is the news-pro Apple TV+ register territory (T-330 `apple-tv-lt`).
- Atmospheric companion clips (grain / light-leak / particles / photographic-overlay) — cluster-D prestige-TV territory, NOT cluster-F creator-economy prestige territory. The editorial-magazine canvas stays clean.
- Per-shot transition animations (the within-shot per-letter entry from `'letterform-assemble'`, the hard-cut color-jumps from `'palette-jump-cut'`) — out of v1 scope; v1 is the steady-state `'plate-and-credits'` register.
- Bespoke `GT Sectra` proprietary-byo wiring — production-fidelity preference, BYO-license-gated at deploy time. v1 ships the Cormorant Garamond OFL fallback for the parity render.
- Dedicated cluster-F `fullscreenTitle` primitive — candidate primitive-level carve-out under the `T-520a` / cluster-F compose-tools (T-368) label; v1 reuses the cluster-D `titleSequence` primitive cross-cluster.
