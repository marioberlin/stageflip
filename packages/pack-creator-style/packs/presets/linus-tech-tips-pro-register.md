# Linus Tech Tips Pro — lower third

## Visual tokens
- Bar: solid white `#FFFFFF` fill — the canonical LTT energetic-broadcast canvas, the visual signature carried across LTT's on-screen overlays since the channel's broadcast-pace tech-reviewer canon. Pure white is the LTT-pro register; near-black `#1A1A1A` belongs to the broadcast scoring-strip Cluster B (T-512..T-515), pure black `#000000` belongs to T-517 MKBHD Pro (premium-tech-reviewer cinematic register), and `#080f15` is the `LowerThird` primitive's near-black default. LTT-pro shares the white-canvas signature with T-518 Vox-deluxe — the LTT and Vox registers BOTH invert MKBHD's black bar — but the distinguishing visual signatures (LTT Blue strip + UPPERCASE attribution + slide animation; Vox Yellow strip + Mixed-Case attribution + fade animation) separate the two within the cluster-F register family. The `LowerThird` primitive (T-183) accepts `background` as a single hex string with no opacity channel; v1 renders at 100 % opacity on the flat-color parity canvas. LTT production overlays run the bar at 100 % over broadcast-pace tech-reviewer B-roll for high-contrast energetic posture — no production opacity tunable applies here (see "Trade-offs").
- Accent strip: LTT Blue `#0080FF` — rendered as a 6 px-wide flex child on the **left edge** of the composite (`borderRadius: 3`); this electric-blue strip IS the LTT brand identifier and ties the chyron to the channel's broader on-air identity (channel-art highlight, on-screen overlay accent, end-card chevron all use the same `#0080FF`). Never paint the full bar blue. Distinct from T-517's MKBHD Red `#E63946` strip and T-518's Vox Yellow `#FFE800` strip — the LTT blue is the channel's signature electric primary, leaning pure-saturated blue against pure white. The blue-strip-on-white combination is the LTT visual signature observed across the channel wordmark canon.
- Headline color `#000000` (black on white) — the highest-contrast reading on the white bar. Subtitle color renders in `#0080FF` (LTT Blue — see "Trade-offs" below; not an independently colorable channel-host token under the primitive's prop surface unless `subtitleColor` is wired). The text inversion (black-on-white) matches T-518 Vox-deluxe — both white-canvas registers share the high-contrast dark-text-on-light reading, distinguishing them from MKBHD's white-on-black cinematic posture.
- Bar anchored bottom-left at `insetLeftPx: 64`, `insetBottomPx: 56` — matches the T-517 / T-518 / T-507 Sky News Pro asymmetric left-aligned anchor norm, intermediate between BBC Reith's `insetBottomPx: 48` (sits visually lowest) and CNN-Classic's `insetBottomPx: 64`. LTT broadcast canon places the title strip a touch above the lower-frame margin reserved for the subscribe-prompt + product-on-desk b-roll stack. Asymmetric left-aligned, NOT center-anchored — broadcast-pace tech-reviewer canon is cinematic-third with broadcast urgency, not center-strip.
- Card auto-fits the headline against a `minWidth: 240` floor with `padding: '12px 20px'` and uniform `borderRadius: 6` — the primitive's default geometry reads correctly against the LTT-pro register. LTT broadcasts occasionally use a sharper rectangular card with no corner softening; the primitive renders all four corners at uniform 6 px (see "Trade-offs").
- v1 uses the existing `LowerThird` primitive (T-183) prop surface: `background`, `accent`, `textColor`, `name`, `title`, `insetLeftPx`, `insetBottomPx`. Snapshot constants live in `LTT_PRO_REGISTER_PROPS` (exported from `@stageflip/parity-cli` at the cluster sign-off step).

## Typography
- Headline (`name`): Mixed Case applied at the snapshot string level (`'iPhone 17 Pro vs Galaxy S25 Ultra'`). Rendered at the primitive's default 34 px / fontWeight 700 — within the broadcast-pace tech-reviewer register's 32–36 pt headline range. Mixed Case (NOT UPPERCASE) is the LTT-pro register's signature for the headline slot — LTT product comparisons follow the manufacturer's own casing (`iPhone`, `Galaxy`, `MacBook`, etc.); the headline is editorial Mixed Case, not broadcast-news UPPERCASE. UPPERCASE headlines would read as broadcast-news (T-507 Sky News / T-508 ITV / T-509 RAI register) and migrate the visual off the broadcast-pace tech-reviewer cluster entirely.
- Subtitle (`title`): UPPERCASE channel/host name (`'LINUS SEBASTIAN'`); rendered at 18 px / fontWeight 500. The channel wordmark + host attribution canon at LTT is canonically uppercase + wide-tracked — matches MKBHD's wordmark register (T-517) and differs from Vox-deluxe's Mixed-Case attribution (T-518). LTT splits the cluster-F typography difference: Mixed Case editorial headline (Vox posture) + UPPERCASE wide-tracked attribution (MKBHD posture); this is the distinguishing typographic signature of the LTT-pro register. The primitive's hard-coded `letterSpacing: '0.02em'` on the title line approximates the wordmark's wide tracking (see "Trade-offs" — the wordmark's production tracking is wider than the primitive's default).
- Rendered family v1: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (the primitive's hard-coded inline `fontFamily`). The bespoke `LTT Display` (proprietary BYO; the channel's bespoke display family used on-air, modeled on the LTT channel-art display face) and the OFL fallback `Inter 700` declared in frontmatter exist for the type-design batch review (sister cluster-F composer task). Adding a `font.family` prop to the `LowerThird` primitive remains a candidate `T-183z` primitive-level follow-up — the prop already exists per T-183z schema (lines 55–65), but is not wired into the rendered output by the existing primitive's CSS without the carve-out work.
- Letter-spacing: the primitive hard-codes `letterSpacing: '-0.015em'` on the name (tight Mixed-Case headline) and `letterSpacing: '0.02em'` on the title (open uppercase channel-host attribution). LTT broadcast typography uses a similar tight-name + open-wordmark posture — the primitive's defaults align with the register; only the attribution's production tracking is wider than the primitive can express without a carve-out (see "Trade-offs").

## Animation
- Slide-in from the left over `ceil(fps * 0.30)` frames easing `EASE_OUT_QUART`; mid-shot hold; slide-out to the right over the last `ceil(fps * 0.25)` frames easing `EASE_IN_QUART` (per the `LowerThird` primitive contract from T-183). At 30 fps: ~300 ms entrance, ~250 ms exit — broadcast-pace tech-reviewer tempo, FASTER than T-517 MKBHD-pro's 450 ms / 350 ms cinematic pacing and T-518 Vox-deluxe's 450 ms / 350 ms magazine-explainer pacing, closer to T-512 NBA Pro's 350 ms / 250 ms broadcast scoring-strip tempo. LTT's energetic broadcast-pace IS the distinguishing animation signature within the cluster-F register family.
- Steady-state mid-hold: `translatePct == 0`, `opacity == 1`. The parity golden captures this state at frame 60 (= 2000 ms @ 30 fps composition) — matches T-507 / T-508 / T-509 / T-512 / T-513 / T-514 / T-515 / T-517 / T-518 cross-cluster lower-third + score-bug parity-fixture frame-pinning norm.
- The spec's canonical LTT-pro pacing target is "300 ms in, 2.5-second mid-hold, 250 ms out". The primitive's mid-hold is implicit — `translatePct` settles at 0 after frame `enterEnd` and stays there until `exitStart`; the composition's `durationInFrames` controls the total hold. The parity-fixture composition pins `durationInFrames` to 75 (= 2500 ms @ 30 fps) by default for LTT; the 2.5-second mid-hold target falls out naturally from the composition's clip-envelope duration, not from a primitive-level prop.
- The primitive's slide+fade entrance (opacity interpolates 0→1 alongside the slide-in `translatePct`) reads correctly as the LTT-pro broadcast-pace slide entrance — the slide motion IS the distinguishing animation signature here (vs T-517 + T-518's fade-only intent that nonetheless ships as slide+fade per the primitive's hard-coded animation contract). For LTT-pro the slide IS the spec, not a trade-off — the primitive's slide+fade entrance matches the spec's canonical "slide-in" target directly.

## Rules
- Use when a broadcast-pace tech-reviewer energetic title register is called for — product-comparison intro cards, fast-pace tech-review attribution lines, broadcast-style overlay cards on tech-reviewer documentary content. LTT-pro register sits within Cluster F's creator-economy family next to T-517 MKBHD-pro (substantive; premium-tech-reviewer cinematic), T-518 Vox-deluxe (substantive; magazine-explainer humanist), T-520 prestige-creator composition preset (still placeholder). Choose LTT-pro for the broadcast-pace energetic tech-reviewer register specifically — fast-pace product comparisons, on-screen overlay cards, energetic broadcast canon.
- Do not use for broadcast-news contexts (use a Cluster A `lowerThird` preset like `sky-news-pro-register` T-507 / `itv-pro-register` T-508 / `rai-pro-register` T-509 / `cnn-classic` T-323 / `bbc-reith-dark` T-325).
- Do not use for sports-broadcast scoring-strip contexts (use a Cluster B `scoreBug` preset like `nba-pro-register` T-512 / `nfl-pro-register` T-513 / `mlb-register` T-514 / `f1-pro-register` T-515).
- Do not use for premium-tech-reviewer cinematic-third contexts (use T-517 `mkbhd-pro-register` instead — black bar + red strip + white text is the MKBHD register, not LTT-pro).
- Do not use for magazine-explainer long-form documentary contexts (use T-518 `vox-deluxe-register` instead — white bar + yellow strip + Mixed-Case attribution + fade is the Vox-deluxe register, not LTT-pro).
- Do not paint the full bar blue. The 6 px-wide LTT Blue `#0080FF` accent strip on the left is the brand identifier — pulling the strip to black (to recover a black-subtitle stub spec) would destroy the brand signal, and pulling the bar to blue would saturate the broadcast register and read as institutional-broadcaster (Cluster A) rather than creator-economy.
- Mixed Case headline + UPPERCASE channel-host attribution — NOT both Mixed-Case (Vox-deluxe register T-518) or both UPPERCASE (broadcast-news Cluster A territory). The split casing IS the LTT-pro typographic signature.
- Do not substitute the blue strip for any other accent color. The strip IS the brand mark and ties the chyron to LTT's broader on-air identity (channel-art highlight, on-screen overlay accent, end-card chevron all use the same `#0080FF`).
- Animation tempo IS the LTT signature — 300 ms / 250 ms broadcast-pace slides, NOT MKBHD/Vox's 450 / 350 ms cinematic-third fades. Slowing the tempo to 450 / 350 ms migrates the visual onto T-517 or T-518 register territory.
- Designed for broadcast-pace tech-reviewer on-air talent and product-title identification across the full LTT catalog — same legibility requirements as the Cluster A news-broadcaster register (subtitle ≥ 18 pt; primitive's 18 px default satisfies).

## Trade-offs (v1 cosmetic divergences from the compass register)
- **Attribution text renders blue, not the canonical LTT broadcast white-or-black.** The `LowerThird` primitive (T-183) renders the optional `title` line at `color: subtitleColor ?? accent` (lower-third.tsx:91). With `accent: '#0080FF'` and `subtitleColor` unset, the attribution reads in LTT Blue on the white bar — the spec's canonical attribution color is dark-grey or black on the white canvas. v1 ships with the primitive's default subtitle-falls-back-to-accent rendering; setting `subtitleColor: '#000000'` would recover the canonical dark attribution and is preferred at the cluster-F composer task's binding-wire step. The trade-off here is conservative: v1 leaves the existing primitive call site unchanged from the placeholder shape. Same trade-off as T-517 MKBHD + T-518 Vox + T-507 Sky News + T-323 CNN-Classic + T-325 BBC-Reith-Dark documented (the symmetric pattern across cluster-F where the subtitle inherits accent-on-canvas where canon is contrast-on-canvas).
- **Rendered family is `Plus Jakarta Sans`, not the proprietary `LTT Display` or the OFL fallback `Inter 700`.** The primitive hard-codes `fontFamily: 'Plus Jakarta Sans, sans-serif'` (line 88). The proprietary `LTT Display` (BYO) and the OFL fallback `Inter` declared in frontmatter exist for the type-design batch review's evaluation of declared fonts and are not honored at render time. The primitive's `font.family` prop (T-183z, lines 55–65) is wired into the schema but the rendered CSS does not yet use it; wiring it through is a candidate `T-183z`-family follow-up under the same label.
- **Attribution letter-spacing is `0.02em`, not the production wider tracking.** The primitive hard-codes `letterSpacing: '0.02em'` on the title line. LTT's production attribution wordmark uses a wider tracking (~0.08–0.12em range) on the channel's on-air cards (matches MKBHD's wide-wordmark canon, T-517). v1 accepts the primitive's default; adding a `subtitleLetterSpacingEm?` prop is a candidate primitive-level follow-up under the `T-183z`-family label.
- **Uniform 6 px border radius, not pure-rectangular sharp corners.** The primitive's card uses `borderRadius: 6` on all four corners. LTT's broadcast title strips occasionally feature pure rectangular cards with no corner softening (broadcast-pace tech-reviewer aesthetic); v1 uses uniform 6 px — close to the canonical intent; the visual cluster identity (white bar + blue strip + split-case typography + broadcast-pace slide) reads correctly at 6 px uniform. Per-corner radii (or a `borderRadius: 0` opt-in) is a candidate primitive-level follow-up under the same `T-183z`-family label.
- **Mid-hold duration is composition-controlled, not primitive-pinned.** The spec calls for a 2.5-second mid-hold between the 300 ms entrance and 250 ms exit. The `LowerThird` primitive does not expose a mid-hold duration prop; the mid-hold falls out of the composition's `durationInFrames`. The parity-fixture composition pins `durationInFrames: 75` (= 2500 ms @ 30 fps) to match. Adding an explicit `holdDurationFrames` prop is a candidate primitive-level follow-up under the same label.
