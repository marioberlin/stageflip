# Vox Deluxe — lower third

## Visual tokens
- Bar: solid white `#FFFFFF` fill — the canonical Vox explainer-doc canvas, the visual signature carried across every long-form explainer card in the Vox / Vox Media catalog since the channel's magazine-explainer reposition. Pure white is the Vox-deluxe register; near-black `#1A1A1A` belongs to the broadcast scoring-strip Cluster B (T-512..T-515), pure black `#000000` belongs to T-517 MKBHD Pro (premium-tech-reviewer cinematic register), and `#080f15` is the `LowerThird` primitive's near-black default. The inversion (white-on-Vox vs black-on-MKBHD) IS the visual signature that distinguishes the two cluster-F register slots — Vox-deluxe is the magazine-explainer register, MKBHD-pro is the premium-tech-reviewer register. The `LowerThird` primitive (T-183) accepts `background` as a single hex string with no opacity channel; v1 renders at 100 % opacity on the flat-color parity canvas. Vox production cards run the bar at 100 % over documentary B-roll for high-contrast magazine posture — no production opacity tunable applies here (see "Trade-offs").
- Accent strip: Vox Yellow `#FFE800` — rendered as a 6 px-wide flex child on the **left edge** of the composite (`borderRadius: 3`); this yellow strip IS the Vox brand identifier and ties the chyron to the Vox.com brand-identity highlight color. Never paint the full bar yellow. Distinct from any broadcaster red/blue strip (Cluster A) and from T-517's MKBHD Red `#E63946` strip — the Vox yellow is the channel's signature accent against pure white, leaning pure-primary yellow with no warm cast.
- Headline color `#000000` (black on white) — the highest-contrast reading on the white bar. Subtitle color renders in `#FFE800` (Vox Yellow — see "Trade-offs" below; not an independently colorable channel-host token under the primitive's prop surface unless `subtitleColor` is wired). The text inversion (black-on-white vs MKBHD's white-on-black) follows directly from the canvas inversion — Vox's magazine-explainer canon is high-contrast dark-text-on-light, opposite of MKBHD's cinematic dark-canvas-with-light-text.
- Bar anchored bottom-left at `insetLeftPx: 64`, `insetBottomPx: 56` — matches the T-517 MKBHD-pro + T-507 Sky News Pro asymmetric left-aligned anchor norm, intermediate between BBC Reith's `insetBottomPx: 48` (sits visually lowest) and CNN-Classic's `insetBottomPx: 64`. Vox explainer-doc canon places the title strip a touch above the lower-frame margin reserved for the chapter-prompt + on-screen-graphic stack typical of long-form explainer pieces. Asymmetric left-aligned, NOT center-anchored — magazine-explainer canon is cinematic-third, not broadcast-strip.
- Card auto-fits the headline against a `minWidth: 240` floor with `padding: '12px 20px'` and uniform `borderRadius: 6` — the primitive's default geometry reads correctly against the Vox-deluxe register. Vox explainer cards occasionally use a sharper rectangular card with no corner softening; the primitive renders all four corners at uniform 6 px (see "Trade-offs").
- v1 uses the existing `LowerThird` primitive (T-183) prop surface: `background`, `accent`, `textColor`, `name`, `title`, `insetLeftPx`, `insetBottomPx`. Snapshot constants live in `VOX_DELUXE_REGISTER_PROPS` (exported from `@stageflip/parity-cli` at the cluster sign-off step).

## Typography
- Headline (`name`): Mixed Case applied at the snapshot string level (`'How TikTok rewired the internet'`). Rendered at the primitive's default 34 px / fontWeight 700 — within the magazine-explainer register's 32–36 pt headline range. Mixed Case (NOT UPPERCASE) is the Vox-deluxe register's signature for the title slot — Vox's editorial voice is humanist sentence-case, and headlines on explainer cards follow editorial casing conventions (`'How TikTok rewired the internet'`, `'The hidden cost of cheap clothes'`, `'Why the West gets the future wrong'`). UPPERCASE titles would read as broadcast-news (T-507 Sky News / T-508 ITV / T-509 RAI register) and migrate the visual off the magazine-explainer cluster entirely.
- Subtitle (`title`): Mixed Case host/reporter attribution (`'Joss Fong, Senior Reporter'`); rendered at 18 px / fontWeight 500. Vox attributes its explainer-doc segments to the reporter by full name + role in Mixed Case sentence-case across every long-form piece — opposite of MKBHD's UPPERCASE wordmark register. The primitive's hard-coded `letterSpacing: '0.02em'` on the title line is acceptable for a Mixed Case attribution line; the magazine-explainer canon does not demand wide tracking (see "Trade-offs" — the production tracking on a Mixed-Case attribution line typically sits near zero, but 0.02em is close enough to read correctly).
- Rendered family v1: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (the primitive's hard-coded inline `fontFamily`). The bespoke `Vox Display` (proprietary BYO; the channel's bespoke display family used on-air, modeled on the Vox.com editorial display face) and the OFL fallback `Inter 700` declared in frontmatter exist for the type-design batch review (sister cluster-F composer task). Adding a `font.family` prop to the `LowerThird` primitive remains a candidate `T-183z` primitive-level follow-up — the prop already exists per T-183z schema (lines 55–65), but is not wired into the rendered output by the existing primitive's CSS without the carve-out work.
- Letter-spacing: the primitive hard-codes `letterSpacing: '-0.015em'` on the name (tight Mixed-Case headline) and `letterSpacing: '0.02em'` on the title (slightly open Mixed-Case attribution). Vox magazine-explainer typography uses a tight headline + near-zero attribution tracking posture — the primitive's defaults align with the register; only the attribution line's production tracking is slightly tighter than the primitive can express without a carve-out (see "Trade-offs").

## Animation
- Slide-in from the left over `ceil(fps * 0.45)` frames easing `EASE_OUT_QUART`; mid-shot hold; slide-out to the right over the last `ceil(fps * 0.35)` frames easing `EASE_IN_QUART` (per the `LowerThird` primitive contract from T-183). At 30 fps: ~450 ms entrance, ~350 ms exit — magazine-explainer pacing is the BBC humanist tempo, slower than broadcast-news scoring-strip pacing (T-512 NBA Pro's 350 ms / 250 ms) and matched to T-517 MKBHD-pro's tech-reviewer pacing.
- Steady-state mid-hold: `translatePct == 0`, `opacity == 1`. The parity golden captures this state at frame 60 (= 2000 ms @ 30 fps composition) — matches T-507 / T-508 / T-509 / T-512 / T-513 / T-514 / T-515 / T-517 cross-cluster lower-third + score-bug parity-fixture frame-pinning norm.
- The spec's canonical Vox-deluxe pacing target is "450 ms in, 3-second mid-hold, 350 ms out". The primitive's mid-hold is implicit — `translatePct` settles at 0 after frame `enterEnd` and stays there until `exitStart`; the composition's `durationInFrames` controls the total hold. The parity-fixture composition pins `durationInFrames` to 90 (= 3000 ms @ 30 fps) by default; the 3-second mid-hold target falls out naturally from the composition's clip-envelope duration, not from a primitive-level prop.
- Fade-in is implicit in the primitive's opacity ramp (lines 107–118: opacity interpolates 0→1 alongside the slide-in `translatePct`); the spec's "fade-in" reads as the opacity ramp synchronized with the slide-in entrance. v1 accepts the primitive's slide+fade entrance as the Vox-deluxe pacing target match. Pure fade-only entrance (no horizontal travel) is a candidate primitive-level follow-up under the `T-183z`-family label.

## Rules
- Use when a magazine-explainer long-form documentary title register is called for — explainer-doc intro cards, reporter attribution lines, long-form-magazine title strips. Vox-deluxe register sits within Cluster F's creator-economy family next to T-517 MKBHD-pro (substantive), T-519 Linus-Tech-Tips-pro (still placeholder), T-520 prestige-creator composition preset (still placeholder). Choose Vox-deluxe for the magazine-explainer documentary register specifically.
- Do not use for broadcast-news contexts (use a Cluster A `lowerThird` preset like `sky-news-pro-register` T-507 / `itv-pro-register` T-508 / `rai-pro-register` T-509 / `cnn-classic` T-323 / `bbc-reith-dark` T-325).
- Do not use for sports-broadcast scoring-strip contexts (use a Cluster B `scoreBug` preset like `nba-pro-register` T-512 / `nfl-pro-register` T-513 / `mlb-register` T-514 / `f1-pro-register` T-515).
- Do not use for premium-tech-reviewer cinematic-third contexts (use T-517 `mkbhd-pro-register` instead — black bar + red strip + white text is the MKBHD register, not Vox-deluxe).
- Do not paint the full bar yellow. The 6 px-wide Vox Yellow `#FFE800` accent strip on the left is the brand identifier — pulling the strip to black (to recover a black-subtitle stub spec) would destroy the brand signal, and pulling the bar to yellow would saturate the magazine-explainer register and read as caution-tape rather than editorial canvas.
- Mixed Case headline + Mixed Case attribution — NOT uppercase. Uppercase headlines read as broadcast-news and migrate the visual onto Cluster A territory; the Vox-deluxe register is editorial sentence-case magazine-explainer.
- Do not substitute the yellow strip for any other accent color. The strip IS the brand mark and ties the chyron to Vox's broader on-air identity (logo-mark accent, lower-third highlight, chapter-card chevron all use the same `#FFE800`).
- Designed for magazine-explainer on-air reporters and documentary-title identification across the full Vox catalog — same legibility requirements as the Cluster A news-broadcaster register (subtitle ≥ 18 pt; primitive's 18 px default satisfies).

## Acceptance (parity)
- Reference frame: 60 (= 2000 ms @ 30 fps; steady-state mid-hold; bar + strip + headline + attribution fully settled).

## Trade-offs (v1 cosmetic divergences from the compass register)
- **Attribution text renders yellow, not the canonical Vox sentence-case dark-grey.** The `LowerThird` primitive (T-183) renders the optional `title` line at `color: subtitleColor ?? accent` (lower-third.tsx:91). With `accent: '#FFE800'` and `subtitleColor` unset, the attribution reads in Vox Yellow on the white bar — the spec's canonical attribution color is dark-grey or black. v1 ships with the primitive's default subtitle-falls-back-to-accent rendering; setting `subtitleColor: '#000000'` would recover the canonical dark attribution and is preferred at the cluster-F composer task's binding-wire step. The trade-off here is conservative: v1 leaves the existing primitive call site unchanged from the placeholder shape. Same trade-off as T-517 MKBHD + T-507 Sky News + T-323 CNN-Classic + T-325 BBC-Reith-Dark documented (the symmetric inversion of T-517: there the subtitle inherits red-on-black where canon is white; here it inherits yellow-on-white where canon is black).
- **Rendered family is `Plus Jakarta Sans`, not the proprietary `Vox Display` or the OFL fallback `Inter 700`.** The primitive hard-codes `fontFamily: 'Plus Jakarta Sans, sans-serif'` (line 88). The proprietary `Vox Display` (BYO) and the OFL fallback `Inter` declared in frontmatter exist for the type-design batch review's evaluation of declared fonts and are not honored at render time. The primitive's `font.family` prop (T-183z, lines 55–65) is wired into the schema but the rendered CSS does not yet use it; wiring it through is a candidate `T-183z`-family follow-up under the same label.
- **Attribution letter-spacing is `0.02em`, not the production near-zero tracking.** The primitive hard-codes `letterSpacing: '0.02em'` on the title line. Vox's production attribution typography uses near-zero tracking (~0em range) on the channel's magazine-explainer cards — the editorial Mixed-Case attribution sits tighter than uppercase wordmarks. v1 accepts the primitive's default; adding a `subtitleLetterSpacingEm?` prop is a candidate primitive-level follow-up under the `T-183z`-family label.
- **Uniform 6 px border radius, not pure-rectangular sharp corners.** The primitive's card uses `borderRadius: 6` on all four corners. Vox's magazine-explainer title strips occasionally feature pure rectangular cards with no corner softening (long-form documentary aesthetic); v1 uses uniform 6 px — close to the canonical intent; the visual cluster identity (white bar + yellow strip + Mixed-Case typography) reads correctly at 6 px uniform. Per-corner radii (or a `borderRadius: 0` opt-in) is a candidate primitive-level follow-up under the same `T-183z`-family label.
- **Mid-hold duration is composition-controlled, not primitive-pinned.** The spec calls for a 3-second mid-hold between the 450 ms entrance and 350 ms exit. The `LowerThird` primitive does not expose a mid-hold duration prop; the mid-hold falls out of the composition's `durationInFrames`. The parity-fixture composition pins `durationInFrames: 90` (= 3000 ms @ 30 fps) to match. Adding an explicit `holdDurationFrames` prop is a candidate primitive-level follow-up under the same label.
