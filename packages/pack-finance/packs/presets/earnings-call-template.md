# Earnings-call template — quarterly results announcement composition

First substantive preset in the Earnings & Investor pack (skeleton landed T-521; closes the first of four placeholder slots opened by T-521). UNLIKE the cluster-A / cluster-B / cluster-F register presets (Sky News / NBA Pro / MKBHD / Vox / LTT etc.) which key a single brand to a single clipKind for a strip-style lower-third register, this preset is a **composition template** for a specific financial-communication USE CASE — the "results-announcement" video CEOs / IR teams produce in the hour after the earnings call concludes. Multi-shot composition binding multiple existing clipKinds via PRESET_ID_BINDINGS Pattern C: cluster-D `titleSequence` for the cover slide, cluster-A `lowerThird` for KPI supers / CEO commentary / Q&A speaker tags. The visual register is conservative-corporate (deep institutional blue / serif headline / sans-serif body metrics) — NOT broadcast-news (Sky News / CNN), NOT broadcast-sports (NBA / NFL), NOT prestige-TV (Stranger Things / GoT) and NOT prestige-creator (Vanity Fair / Apple TV+ documentary editorial). Earnings-call is the FINANCIAL-COMMUNICATION register, a register-cluster of its own that the cluster-finance metadata cluster captures.

## Visual tokens

The earnings-call register is conservative-corporate and locked: deep institutional-blue full-bleed canvas across every shot, white serif headline on dark, sans-serif body metrics, forward-guidance green for positive numerical deltas, brick red for negative numerical deltas. The register is the post-call investor-relations canon shared across Apple, Microsoft, Alphabet, Meta, and Amazon's quarterly-results announcement videos (the 90-second post-earnings clip CEOs publish to IR pages).

- **Background**: deep institutional blue `#0F172A` (Tailwind slate-900) — full-bleed cinematic canvas across every shot in the composition. NOT the near-black `#1A1A1A` broadcast-strip register (Cluster B sports / Cluster A news-ticker territory), NOT the pure-black `#000000` editorial-magazine register (T-520 prestige-creator territory), NOT the off-white `#F5F1E8` magazine-explainer register (T-518 Vox-deluxe territory). The slate-900 institutional blue IS the financial-communication register — Bloomberg Terminal's primary backdrop sits in the same hue-family, and the dark-blue-on-white-text contrast pattern carries the gravitas the financial-broadcast use case demands. Same backdrop across all five shots in the composition (cover / KPI / commentary / Q&A / guidance) — no per-shot palette divergence.
- **Foreground / primary text color**: `#FFFFFF` — white. Maximum-contrast white text on the deep-blue canvas; cross-register financial-broadcast canon for headline + body slots both.
- **Positive-delta numerical highlight**: `#10B981` (Tailwind emerald-500) — forward-guidance green. Reserved EXCLUSIVELY for positive numerical deltas (revenue beat, EPS beat, guidance raise, YoY growth). NEVER use green for non-numerical content. The discipline matches SEC Reg FD informal-disclosure convention — color-coding within the numerical-metric slot is fair-disclosure-compliant; color-coding within the narrative-commentary slot reads as editorial editorialization and migrates the register onto opinion-broadcast territory.
- **Negative-delta numerical highlight**: `#DC2626` (Tailwind red-600) — brick red. Reserved EXCLUSIVELY for negative numerical deltas (revenue miss, EPS miss, guidance cut, YoY decline). NEVER use red for non-numerical content. The same Reg-FD discipline applies — color signaling stays inside the numerical-metric slot.
- **No accent color outside the green/red numerical slot.** The register is intentionally austere — no third accent hue, no atmospheric overlay, no gradient. The conservative-corporate canon IS the cleanliness; adding a third accent migrates the visual onto cluster-A broadcast-news (`#E40523` Sky News red) or cluster-B broadcast-sports (`#F97316` Premier League orange) territory.
- **Layout** — five-shot composition per the §Composition structure section below; each shot uses the `lowerThird` primitive's bottom-anchored strip-register (cover slide uses `titleSequence`'s centered full-frame register). The strip-register positions are pinned at `position: { x: 80, y: 580, width: 1120, alignment: 'left' }` at 1280×720 (the parity-CLI's fixed `DEFAULT_COMPOSITION`) — left-anchored cinematic-third strip, NOT centered, NOT right-anchored.
- **No atmospheric overlays, no grain, no light-leaks, no particle drift.** The financial-communication canon is clean type on a clean canvas — adding atmospheric overlays migrates the visual onto cluster-D prestige-TV title territory (T-348 stranger-things-benguiat / T-351 true-detective-double-exposure).

The five-shot sequence IS the message — at the parity-fixture reference frame the eye locks onto the active shot's mid-hold steady-state, framed entirely by the deep-blue institutional canvas. The leisurely pacing (600 ms fade-in → 4-second mid-hold → 500 ms fade-out per shot) is the post-Reg-FD canonical UX gesture for serious financial communication — broadcast-pace is too fast for the comprehension load (revenue figures, EPS deltas, forward-guidance ranges all need reading time).

## Typography

- **`preferredFont: GT Sectra`** (`proprietary-byo` — Grilli Type's editorial-magazine display serif). Same preferred family as T-520 prestige-creator; the transitional / didone hybrid construction with its sharp wedge-serif terminals and high-contrast modulation IS the financial-broadcast prestige register. Cluster F's type-design batch covers the cluster-finance type sign-off by precedent (cluster-F prestige-creator uses GT Sectra and is the closest sibling register); cluster-finance type-design batch review is the cluster-composer task downstream. `signOff.typeDesign` MUST be `'pending-cluster-batch'` or `'signed:YYYY-MM-DD'`, NOT `'na'`.
- **`fallbackFont: Cormorant Garamond 700`** (`ofl` — SIL Open Font License 1.1 via Google Fonts). Cormorant Garamond is the OFL transitional-serif-revival closest to GT Sectra in construction — sharp wedge serifs, high contrast modulation, narrow proportions. Same OFL family used as the cluster-D stranger-things-benguiat fallback (T-348), got-trajan-clockwork fallback (T-349), and cluster-F prestige-creator fallback (T-520) — the canonical OFL transitional-serif-revival fallback for prestige-typography registers. Type-design consultant approval on the GT Sectra → Cormorant Garamond fallback path is pending the cluster-finance type-design batch review per ADR-004 §D4.
- **Headline (`titlePlate.content.text` for the cover slide; `lowerThird.content.title` for KPI / commentary / Q&A / guidance shots)**: Mixed Case at the snapshot string level (`'Q4 FY2026 Results'`, `'Revenue $94.9B (+8% YoY)'` etc.). Rendered via `font: { family, weight: 700, size: 48, letterSpacing: -0.01 }` at the schema-prop level on the cover slide (60 px on the cover slide reads as too cinematic for the corporate register — pulled to 48 px); 36 px / fontWeight 600 for the KPI / commentary / Q&A / guidance shot titles. Mixed Case (NOT UPPERCASE) IS the financial-broadcast register — Apple / Microsoft / Alphabet / Meta / Amazon quarterly-results video title cards all run Mixed Case headline bodies. UPPERCASE headlines would migrate the visual onto Cluster A broadcast-news (Sky News / ITV / RAI register) or American-tabloid (CNN-breaking) territory entirely.
- **Body / metric numerals (`lowerThird.content.subtitle` slot for KPI / guidance shots)**: Plus Jakarta Sans 500 / 28 px / letterSpacing 0 — sans-serif body register for the numerical-metric slot. The serif-headline + sans-body split is the financial-broadcast canonical typographic signature (Bloomberg / FT / WSJ all use the same split). Plus Jakarta Sans is the OFL geometric-humanist sans-serif (Google Fonts; OFL 1.1) closest in construction to the bespoke corporate-sans families Apple uses for its IR communications (SF Pro Display) and Microsoft uses for its IR communications (Segoe UI Display); cluster-finance batch-review can flip to a bespoke `proprietary-byo` corporate-sans for production deployments.
- **Speaker-tag (`lowerThird.content.subtitle` slot for commentary / Q&A shots)**: Plus Jakarta Sans 500 / 22 px (smaller than KPI metric slot) — the speaker name + title combination (e.g. `'Tim Cook · CEO'`) reads at a sub-headline register beneath the lowerThird title.
- **Casing transform**: `'as-is'` (default). The casing is applied at the snapshot-string level (Mixed Case headlines, Mixed Case body) so the primitive's casing-transform layer is a no-op.
- **No italic, no underline, no strikethrough.** Financial-broadcast typography never uses them — even SEC-mandated disclaimer footer text stays in Mixed Case at a smaller size, not italic.

## Composition structure

Five-shot sequence in declaration order, each shot consuming a single primitive (cluster-D `titleSequence` for shot 1, cluster-A `lowerThird` for shots 2–5):

1. **Cover slide (titleSequence shot, startMs 0, endMs 5500)** — Mixed Case headline `'Q4 FY2026 Results'` centered full-frame on the deep-blue canvas. Style bundle `'plate-and-credits'` (same as T-520 prestige-creator) — the headline plate paired with a credits-block tagline carrying the company name + fiscal quarter (`'ACME CORP · Q4 FY2026 · January 30, 2026'`). Pacing: 600 ms fade-in EASE_OUT_QUART → 4400 ms mid-hold → 500 ms fade-out EASE_IN_QUART.
2. **Key metric — revenue (lowerThird shot, startMs 6000, endMs 11500)** — left-anchored cinematic-third strip. Title `'Revenue $94.9B (+8% YoY)'` with the `(+8% YoY)` delta rendered in forward-guidance green `#10B981`. Subtitle `'Beat consensus $93.2B by $1.7B'` in Plus Jakarta Sans 28 px white. Pacing: 600 ms fade-in EASE_OUT_QUART → 4400 ms mid-hold → 500 ms fade-out EASE_IN_QUART.
3. **CEO commentary (lowerThird shot, startMs 12000, endMs 17500)** — left-anchored cinematic-third strip. Title (the pull-quote) `'Our services business set an all-time record.'` in white serif at 36 px. Subtitle `'Tim Cook · Chief Executive Officer'` in Plus Jakarta Sans 22 px white at 0.6 opacity. Pacing: 600 ms fade-in EASE_OUT_QUART → 4400 ms mid-hold → 500 ms fade-out EASE_IN_QUART.
4. **Q&A clip (lowerThird shot, startMs 18000, endMs 23500)** — left-anchored cinematic-third strip. Title (the analyst question + management response combined) `'Q: Capex trajectory? A: $12B FY2026, flat YoY.'` in white serif at 36 px. Subtitle `'Toni Sacconaghi · Bernstein · Q&A response'` in Plus Jakarta Sans 22 px white at 0.6 opacity. Pacing: 600 ms fade-in EASE_OUT_QUART → 4400 ms mid-hold → 500 ms fade-out EASE_IN_QUART.
5. **Forward guidance footer / disclaimer (lowerThird shot, startMs 24000, endMs 29500)** — left-anchored cinematic-third strip. Title `'FY2027 Guidance: Revenue $390B–$400B'` with the `$390B–$400B` range numerals in forward-guidance green `#10B981` (positive guidance) — would render in brick red `#DC2626` if the range represented a guidance cut. Subtitle `'Forward-looking statement · See 10-Q Risk Factors · Reg FD compliant'` in Plus Jakarta Sans 22 px white at 0.5 opacity (subtler — disclaimer register). Pacing: 600 ms fade-in EASE_OUT_QUART → 4400 ms mid-hold → 500 ms fade-out EASE_IN_QUART.

Total composition duration: 29.5 s (5 shots × ~5.5 s/shot + 500 ms inter-shot gap). The leisurely pacing IS the financial-broadcast register — the comprehension load of revenue figures, EPS deltas, and forward-guidance ranges needs reading time per shot.

## Animation

- **Steady-state register only at the reference frame** per the inherited posture from cluster-A `lowerThird` consumers (T-323 / T-324 / T-325 / T-326 / T-327 / T-328 / T-329 / T-330 / T-507 / T-508 / T-509 / T-510) and cluster-D `titleSequence` consumers (T-348 / T-349 / T-350 / T-351 / T-352 / T-353 / T-520). The composition's active shot at the reference frame renders at full opacity at its final position with no entrance / exit animation active.
- **Choreography target (spec)** — leisurely financial-broadcast pacing across every shot:
  - Fade-in 600 ms EASE_OUT_QUART (primitive default `entrance: 'fade'`)
  - Mid-hold 4400 ms per shot (composition-driven; each shot's `endMs - startMs - entranceMs - exitMs` resolves to 4400 ms)
  - Fade-out 500 ms EASE_IN_QUART (transitionOut `'fade'`, synchronized 500 ms before each shot's `endMs`)
  - Per-shot inter-shot gap 500 ms (the canvas holds the deep-blue backdrop alone between shots) — the consecutive shot fades in over the holding backdrop with no abrupt cut
  - Total per shot: ~5.5 s — faster than T-520 prestige-creator (~6.4 s) but slower than T-517 MKBHD-pro (~3.8 s) and T-519 LTT-pro (~3.05 s); the pacing IS the financial-broadcast canonical UX gesture for serious financial communication
- **Mid-segment steady-state at frame 120** (cluster-norm; the parity-CLI's fixed 150-frame composition window means frame 120 = 4000 ms @ 30 fps falls cleanly in the cover-slide mid-hold window — shot 1 `startMs 0 endMs 5500`). At frame 120 the render captures the cover slide at full opacity with both the headline plate and the credits-block tagline settled at their final positions.
- **No state-transition animation in v1.** State transitions (per-letter staggered entry, per-shot color-jumps, photographic-overlay tinting) belong to other style bundles and other clusters; the earnings-call register is the clean steady-state register.

## Rules

- **Bound primitives**: `titleSequence` for the cover slide + `lowerThird` for the four sub-shots (KPI / commentary / Q&A / guidance). T-522 wires the binding via `PRESET_ID_BINDINGS['earnings-call-template']` per Pattern C. The `'titleSequence'` arm in `DEFAULT_CLIP_KIND_RESOLVER` stays UNCHANGED at the cluster-D squid-game-geometric clipKind-default (T-350); the `'lowerThird'` arm stays at its CNN-classic default (T-323). **First cluster-finance consumer of BOTH `titleSequence` AND `lowerThird` primitives** — the cross-cluster register reuse establishes the cluster-finance vertical-use-case binding the cluster-D + cluster-A primitives in a single composition template.
- **Cross-cluster register reuse: cluster-finance preset binding cluster-D + cluster-A primitives.** The earnings-call register's financial-broadcast canon is materially closer to cluster-D prestige-TV title-card register + cluster-A broadcaster-strip register than to any single existing cluster. Reusing the existing `titleSequence` + `lowerThird` primitives — rather than introducing a new `earningsHeadline` cluster-finance primitive or a new `kpiSuper` cluster-finance primitive — keeps the implementation surface small AND respects the structural-extension rule (§13 of CLAUDE.md): no new clipKind, no new degree-of-freedom on the document or binding model, just a new cluster-finance preset binding existing primitives. The visual register difference (financial-broadcast vs prestige-TV vs broadcaster-strip) is captured in the snapshot constants (`EARNINGS_CALL_TEMPLATE_PROPS`) — same primitives, different props.
- **SEC Reg FD compliance canon.** The register is the post-earnings investor-relations communication canon — Apple / Microsoft / Alphabet / Meta / Amazon quarterly-results video conventions converged on this register because it threads the Reg-FD needle (informal-disclosure-safe color signaling within the numerical slot; serif gravitas matches the 10-Q / 10-K filings; no editorial editorialization in narrative-commentary slot). Choose earnings-call-template for the post-earnings results-announcement video specifically — quarterly-results announcements, IR-page video assets, analyst-day talking-points-summary clips.
- **Color signaling stays inside the numerical-metric slot.** Forward-guidance green `#10B981` and brick red `#DC2626` are reserved EXCLUSIVELY for positive / negative numerical deltas. NEVER use green / red for non-numerical content (narrative commentary, speaker tags, disclaimer footers). The Reg-FD discipline matches financial-broadcast convention — color-coding the narrative-commentary slot reads as editorial editorialization and migrates the register onto opinion-broadcast territory.
- **Mixed Case headline + Mixed Case body — NOT UPPERCASE for either slot.** UPPERCASE headlines migrate the register onto broadcast-news (Sky News / ITV / RAI / CNN-breaking) territory entirely. Apple / Microsoft / Alphabet / Meta / Amazon all run Mixed Case across both slots in their quarterly-results announcement videos; the Mixed-Case-across-the-board register IS the financial-broadcast typographic signature.
- **Leisurely pacing — 600 ms in, 4400 ms mid-hold, 500 ms out per shot.** Total per shot ~5.5 s. Speeding the pacing to 450 / 3000 / 350 ms migrates the register onto cluster-F MKBHD-pro / Vox-deluxe cinematic-third pacing; speeding further to 300 / 2500 / 250 ms migrates onto LTT-pro broadcast-pace pacing. The leisurely financial-broadcast pace IS the earnings-call signature — comprehension load on the numerical slot demands reading time.
- **No atmospheric overlays.** No optical grain, no light-leak, no dust-particle drift, no compositional gradient. The conservative-corporate canvas IS the register; atmospheric overlays migrate the visual onto cluster-D prestige-TV title territory.
- **Designed for serious financial-communication assets** — quarterly-results announcement videos, IR-page video clips, analyst-day summary clips, capital-markets-day announcement videos. Same legibility requirements as the cluster-A news-broadcaster register (headline ≥ 36 pt; primitive's 36–48 px exceeds; subtitle ≥ 18 pt; primitive's 22–28 px exceeds).
- **Reference frame for parity is mid-segment (frame 120)** per cluster-norm — single canonical variant capturing the cover-slide shot at the mid-hold steady-state. The full five-shot composition's parity coverage falls out from the per-shot steady-state register at frame 120 (cover slide is the active shot at frame 120 = 4000 ms @ 30 fps).

## Trade-offs (v1 cosmetic divergences from the compass register)

- **Rendered family is `Cormorant Garamond 700` (OFL fallback), not the bespoke `GT Sectra` (proprietary BYO).** The `titleSequence` primitive's `font.family` prop IS wired into the rendered output, so the rendered family follows the binding's snapshot `font.family` value verbatim. The binding-wire step (cluster-finance composer task) sets `font.family: 'Cormorant Garamond'` for the parity render; flipping to `'GT Sectra'` requires the bespoke font to be BYO-wired at deploy time. v1 ships the OFL fallback verbatim.
- **Headline size 48 px on the cover slide, not the T-520 prestige-creator 60 px range.** The financial-communication register reads at a tighter headline scale than the editorial-magazine register — 48 px reads as conservative-corporate at 1280×720; 60 px reads as cinematic-editorial. Production deployments with a 1920×1080 or 4K canvas can scale up via the binding override.
- **The five-shot composition only exercises the cover-slide shot at the parity reference frame.** At frame 120 = 4000 ms @ 30 fps, only the cover slide (shot 1, startMs 0 endMs 5500) is active; the four `lowerThird` sub-shots (KPI / commentary / Q&A / guidance) are NOT visible in the v1 parity fixture. The parity coverage for the sub-shots falls out of the per-shot steady-state register (each `lowerThird` shot renders identically to a single-shot `lowerThird` preset at its own mid-hold) and is verified via per-shot regression fixtures captured in the cluster-finance composer task downstream. v1 ships the cover-slide steady-state as the canonical reference frame.
- **`stockTicker` clipKind NOT used.** Spec note suggested checking if a `stockTicker` clipKind exists — it does (`packages/runtimes/frame-runtime-bridge/src/clips/stock-ticker.tsx`, `kind: 'stock-ticker'`), but the earnings-call canonical composition does NOT include a candlestick-chart shot. Post-earnings results-announcement videos run KPI numerical-supers (revenue / EPS / guidance ranges) via lower-third strips; the candlestick-chart is a different financial-broadcast use case (intraday-trading-summary clip, market-close recap) and lands in a sister cluster-finance preset under a future `T-522a`-family follow-up. Carving out a `market-close-recap` companion preset is the candidate carve-out under the cluster-finance compose-tools label.
- **No `forward-guidance-range` companion clip carved out.** The forward-guidance shot (shot 5) uses the `lowerThird` primitive's title slot for the guidance range, with the numerals colored forward-guidance green / brick red. A dedicated `forward-guidance-range` primitive (specialized for the upper-bound / lower-bound range register with built-in delta-vs-consensus calc) is a candidate primitive-level carve-out under the cluster-finance compose-tools label; v1 reuses the cluster-A `lowerThird` primitive with the snapshot-level color-coding discipline.
- **`'plate-and-credits'` style overlap window pins both cover-slide shots active at the reference frame.** The headline plate (startMs 0, endMs 5300) and the credits-block tagline (startMs 600, endMs 5500) overlap by 4700 ms. At the reference frame 120 (= 4000 ms @ 30 fps), both shots are active simultaneously. The primitive's `'plate-and-credits'` dispatch renders BOTH active shots layered (the title plate above, the credits block beneath); the v1 parity render captures this two-shot overlap as the steady-state register.
- **No atmospheric companion clips.** Cluster-D titleSequence consumers (stranger-things-benguiat T-348, got-trajan-clockwork T-349 etc.) compose the titleSequence primitive with grain / light-leak / particles / photographic-overlay companion clips for the cluster-D prestige-TV register's analog-film aesthetic. The earnings-call register intentionally OMITS these — the financial-communication canon is clean type on a clean canvas. The binding-wire step composes the titleSequence solo (no `overlays?` array), distinguishing the cluster-finance financial-broadcast register from the cluster-D prestige-TV registers structurally as well as visually.
