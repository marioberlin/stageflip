# T-362 — Preset: `hormozi-montserrat-black` (caption, Cluster F)

**Branch**: `task/T-362-hormozi-montserrat-black`

## Goal

Promote the existing `hormozi-montserrat-black` preset stub from `status: stub` to `status: substantive` and ship it as the **first Cluster F (creator captions) preset to land** AND the **first preset to exercise the just-merged `CaptionClip` primitive (T-316) end-to-end**. The preset captures Alex Hormozi's iconic short-form caption style: heavy black-stroked **Montserrat 800** caps, high-contrast yellow highlight on the active word, fast word-by-word refresh. Common uses: short-form vertical voiceover captions (Reels / TikTok / Shorts), educational + sales/marketing lower-thirds, talking-head + course content. Hormozi's style has become near-universal in modern social-video editing because it survives muted autoplay + thumbnail crops.

T-362 is the **first `caption` clipKind to be wired into the parity-CLI resolver**. The `caption` kind already lives in `VALID_CLIP_KINDS` (per T-316 D-T316-13) and the `'hormozi'` style bundle is already shipped (per T-316 D-T316-2 — Montserrat 800, uppercase, `strokeColor #000000` strokeWidth 6, `highlightColor #FFD60A`, `backdrop none`, `entrance rise`, `staggerMs 80`). What is missing is the resolver entry that maps `clipKind: 'caption'` to a runnable `CaptionClip` mount with a canonical `WordTiming[]` snapshot. T-362 lands that wiring AND the parity-golden sign-off in one PR.

T-362 is **M-sized** — on par with T-360 (`big-number-stat-impact`) and T-356 (`bloomberg-ticker`). Comparable shape: ~15-25 LOC resolver entry + a canonical-snapshot constant + single mid-hold parity golden + sign-off in-PR. The CaptionClip primitive itself is already merged on `main` (PR #321), so unlike T-356 (which carved out `T-356a news-ticker-bar`) and T-358 (which carved out `T-358a outcome-row`), T-362 has **no primitive carve-out** — the gap is closed before the preset PR opens. Inherits T-359a's multi-variant infra in trivial-arity mode, T-359b's prod-renderer pipeline, T-359c's tsx fix, T-360's `(clipKind, presetId)` resolver shape (used in clipKind-default mode here per D-T362-4).

### D-T362-5. Canonical caption text — `"This will change your life forever"`

Six words, plausible Hormozi register (declarative, business / self-improvement adjacent). Mid-length: long enough to fill the ~1536px line at Montserrat 800 size 96 without wrapping awkwardly, short enough that the parity-golden frame catches a clear active-word state with siblings. Cluster owners rebrand at compose time; the preset's contract is the **shape** (word-by-word entrance + active-word highlight + black stroke), not the literal text.

Alternatives considered: `"Stop doing what doesn't work"` (5 words, too short on the row), `"You need to start before you're ready"` (7 words, wraps), `"This will change your life"` (5 words, too few to evidence the stagger). The 6-word phrase exercises the entrance stagger across an even number of words and lands a natural mid-highlight on word 5 at the chosen frame.

### D-T362-6. WordTiming[] shape — 300ms per word; canonical mid-hold at frame 45 (= 1500ms)

Six words at **300 ms each** total **1800 ms**. The mid-hold reference frame is **`frame: 45`** (= `1500 ms` at 30 fps).

Frame timing rationale:
- At `frame: 45`, `currentTimeMs = 1500`. By the primitive's `wordIsVisible` rule (`currentTimeMs >= word.startMs`), all six words have entered.
- By the `wordIsActive` rule (`startMs <= currentTimeMs < endMs`), word 5 (`"life"`, `startMs: 1200, endMs: 1500`) has just exited active state and word 6 (`"forever"`, `startMs: 1500, endMs: 1800`) has just entered active state. **At exactly `currentTimeMs === 1500`**, the strict-less-than upper bound makes `"forever"` the active (highlighted) word.
- Words 1–5 render at `foreground: #FFFFFF` (Hormozi bundle); word 6 renders at `highlightColor: #FFD60A`. The black stroke (`strokeWidth: 6`) wraps every word.
- Frame 45 sits well past the entrance window (`ENTRANCE_FRAMES = 12` per primitive) for every word — entrance rise has fully settled by frame 45 for all words (last-word entrance settled at `1500ms - (5 * 80ms) + 12 frames` = ~1500 - 400 = 1100 ms = frame 33).

**If the renderer evaluates frame 45 as `1499.99ms` due to floating-point drift** and the active-word lands on word 5 instead of word 6, that is acceptable — the parity contract is "ONE word highlighted yellow with all five siblings in white-stroked rest state." The Implementer documents whichever word lands as active in the manifest and PR body. Do NOT shift the frame to "force" word 6 active — frame 45 is the canonical convention; a fractional drift on word boundary is a primitive-side question, not a preset-side fix.

If the rendered golden lands mid-entrance for any word (rare given the math above), shift the canonical frame up to ±15 (range 30–60). Document the choice. Do NOT introduce a second variant — the preset's stylistic point is the active-word highlight, not a per-state palette swap.

### D-T362-11. Background — solid `#0E0E12` for the parity golden; live composition is video underlay

Captions overlay on video at compose time; for the parity golden, render on a solid representative dark backdrop (`#0E0E12` matches the cluster E precedent for dark broadcast registers — used by `bloomberg-ticker` at `#0A0A0A`) so the yellow highlight + black stroke + white foreground all read distinctly. The CaptionClip primitive's container is `backgroundColor: 'transparent'` per `caption.tsx`, so the canvas background bleeds through — the generator's compositor draws the canvas with the background color set by the composition (not by the clip).

In live use, captions sit atop a video stream (talking-head, b-roll, screen recording); the background field of the Hormozi style bundle (`#000000`) is a fallback used only if `backdrop !== 'none'` (which it is — `'none'`). Document this separation in body §Visual tokens: the parity golden's solid backdrop is a documentation choice, not a preset prop.

If the Implementer judges `#0E0E12` produces a confusing composite (e.g., the background color is nearly-equal to the `strokeColor: #000000` and the stroke disappears at the dark edge), substitute a slightly lighter neutral (`#1A1A1F` or `#202024`). Document the choice and the rationale in the manifest.

### D-T362-12. Position — center-screen lower-third; `position: { x: 192, y: 648, width: 1536, alignment: 'center' }`

Captions in Hormozi-style content typically sit center-screen or in the lower third (above the platform UI band on Reels/TikTok/Shorts). Recommendation: **center-aligned in the lower third** of a 1920×1080 canvas:

- `x: 192` — 10% inset from the left edge
- `y: 648` — 60% down the canvas (lower-third anchor; Hormozi videos often crop vertical to 1080×1920 but the parity composition is 1920×1080 horizontal per the established cluster convention)
- `width: 1536` — 80% of canvas width (matches the stub's `## Typography` "4–6 words per line at ~80% frame width" rule)
- `alignment: 'center'` — caption text centered within the position bounds

If the Implementer judges the lower-third position causes the caption to overlap an unexpected element (none expected in a solid-background golden), shift `y` to canvas center (`540`) and document. Do NOT shift `x` or `width` — those are tied to the typography contract.
