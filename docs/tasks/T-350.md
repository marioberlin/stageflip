# T-350 — Preset: `squid-game-geometric` (titleSequence, Cluster D)

**Branch**: `task/T-350-squid-game-geometric`

## Goal

Promote the existing `squid-game-geometric` preset stub from `status: stub` to `status: substantive` and ship it as the **first Cluster D (prestige-TV title sequences) preset to land** AND the **first preset to exercise the just-merged `TitleSequenceClip` primitive (T-321) end-to-end**. The preset captures the Squid Game brutalist geometric register: hot-pink / teal / black hard-cut color jump cuts, ALL-CAPS scaled-to-fill title typography, ○ △ □ symbol identity, ~5 s sequence duration. Common uses: short-form title cards for streaming-platform interludes, brutalist editorial / event branding, high-contrast intro stings.

T-350 is the **first `titleSequence` clipKind to be wired into the parity-CLI resolver**. The `titleSequence` kind is already in `VALID_CLIP_KINDS` (verified `scripts/check-preset-integrity.ts:65`, shipped by T-321 D-T321-11) and the `'palette-jump-cut'` style bundle is already shipped (per T-321 D-T321-3 — hard-cut color panels with optional glyph foreground; cut-only enforced regardless of shot-level `transitionOut`). What is missing is the resolver entry that maps `clipKind: 'titleSequence'` to a runnable `TitleSequenceClip` mount with a canonical shot-array snapshot. T-350 lands that wiring AND the parity-golden sign-off in one PR.

T-350 is **M-sized** — on par with T-362 (`hormozi-montserrat-black`) and T-367 (`karaoke-progressive-wipe`). Comparable shape: ~25–35 LOC resolver entry + a canonical-snapshot constant + single mid-hold parity golden + sign-off in-PR. The TitleSequenceClip primitive itself is already merged on `main` (PR #346, commit `d11807c`), so unlike Cluster F's first wave (which carved out T-322a for primitive bug fixes), T-350 has **no primitive carve-out** — the gap is closed before the preset PR opens. Inherits T-359a's multi-variant infra in trivial-arity mode, T-359b's prod-renderer pipeline, T-359c's tsx fix, T-360's `(clipKind, presetId)` resolver shape (used in clipKind-default mode here per D-T350-6), T-362's first-Cluster-preset workflow (the analog template), T-367's in-PR sign-off (Pattern D from `docs/handover-phase13-mid.md` §2).

## Architectural decisions

### D-T350-1. Compass register match — pink/teal/black hard cuts + ALL-CAPS scaled-to-fill + ○△□ symbols

The stub (lines 23–31, 37–40, 43–46) defines the canonical Squid Game register:

- **Palette**: hot pink / magenta `#E91E63`, teal / dark green `#067162`, black `#000000`, white `#FFFFFF` (lines 24–26).
- **Symbols**: ○ (circle) / △ (triangle) / □ (square) — Korean initials O / J / M for the drama's logo identity (line 27). "Brand signal — must be present in any composition that cites this preset" (line 43).
- **Typography**: ALL CAPS, scaled to fill (line 33). Custom geometric sans-serif preferred; Anton + Bebas Neue stack as OFL fallback (lines 31–32).
- **Animation**: hard-cut palette jumps (`pink → teal → black → pink`); ○△□ symbols enter sequentially with hard-cut snap, 200 ms per symbol (lines 38–39); brutalist pacing (line 41).
- **Rules**: pink-on-teal is iconic; brutalism is the register; "no rounded corners, no shadows, no gradients" (lines 43–45).

T-350 binds these to T-321's `'palette-jump-cut'` style bundle (D-T321-3): cut-only enforced regardless of shot-level `transitionOut`; container bleeds the most-recent `colorPanel` color; `colorPanel.content.glyph` renders the foreground symbol at `font.size * 1.5`. This is a tight match — the bundle was authored with squid-game as its canonical consumer (T-321 lines 47–48).

### D-T350-2. Style bundle — `'palette-jump-cut'`

`style: 'palette-jump-cut'`. Rationale:

- **Cut-only enforcement** (T-321 D-T321-3 line 110; primitive lines 272–276): the brutalist register forbids fades. The bundle hard-codes `effectiveTransition: 'cut'` regardless of `shot.transitionOut`. This matches the stub line 39 ("Color contrast jump cuts — instant, no fades") and line 41 ("Pacing is brutal and contained; do not soften with eases") verbatim.
- **Container background bleed** (primitive lines 718–730): the most-recent `colorPanel.content.color` up to the current ms-time sets the canvas background; whatever shot is active renders foreground on top. This produces the pink/teal/black panel cycling the stub describes (line 39) without needing per-shot wrapper-color plumbing in the snapshot.
- **`colorPanel.content.glyph` render path** (primitive lines 507–527): renders the optional 1..3-char `glyph` foreground at `font.size * 1.5` centered, in `foreground` color. Squid Game's ○△□ symbols slot directly into `glyph` per D-T350-4.

The other three bundles do not fit:
- `'letterform-assemble'` is the Stranger Things scaled-to-viewport per-letter-stagger register; squid-game's pacing is shot-level (panel cuts), not per-letter.
- `'plate-and-credits'` is the Succession / Severance plate-and-credits register; squid-game has no separate credits pass.
- `'photographic-overlay'` is the True Detective typography-over-photo register; squid-game is flat color, not photography.

### D-T350-3. Shot composition — 6-shot timeline, ~5 s @ 30 fps = 150 frames

Per the stub line 37 ("~5 s") and line 38 ("○△□ symbols enter sequentially with hard-cut snap, 200 ms per symbol"). Six-shot timeline:

| # | id | startMs | endMs | kind | content | transitionOut |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | `panel-pink-prelude` | 0 | 600 | `colorPanel` | `{ color: '#E91E63' }` | `cut` |
| 1 | `panel-teal-circle` | 600 | 1200 | `colorPanel` | `{ color: '#067162', glyph: '○' }` | `cut` |
| 2 | `panel-black-triangle` | 1200 | 1800 | `colorPanel` | `{ color: '#000000', glyph: '△' }` | `cut` |
| 3 | `panel-pink-square` | 1800 | 2400 | `colorPanel` | `{ color: '#E91E63', glyph: '□' }` | `cut` |
| 4 | `panel-teal-title` | 2400 | 3600 | `colorPanel` | `{ color: '#067162' }` | `cut` |
| 5 | `title-hold` | 3600 | 5000 | `titlePlate` | `{ text: 'SQUID GAME' }` | `cut` |

Total duration: 5000 ms = 150 frames @ 30 fps.

**Shot 0** (pink prelude, 600 ms) establishes the opening panel color before any symbol enters — the canvas reads pink for the first frame burst, no foreground glyph.

**Shots 1–3** (teal-circle / black-triangle / pink-square, 600 ms each) cycle the three brand symbols against alternating panel colors. The 600 ms cadence exceeds the stub's "200 ms per symbol" entrance — the 200 ms in the stub refers to per-symbol entrance snap WITHIN a panel; the panel itself holds for 600 ms to let the eye rest on each symbol. This is a deliberate divergence: the primitive's `'palette-jump-cut'` bundle does not animate per-symbol entrance, so the per-shot dwell time IS the entrance duration. If the Reviewer pushes for 200 ms per panel (which would compress shots 1–3 into 600 ms total), the canvas reads as a flicker rather than a brand-signal sequence; the 600 ms cadence preserves the brand-signal posture. Document this divergence in the PR body.

**Shot 4** (teal panel, 1200 ms) re-establishes the iconic pink-on-teal background (per stub rule line 44: "Pink-on-teal is the iconic combination") AHEAD of the title plate. Container background changes to teal at ms 2400; the title plate appearing in shot 5 reads on teal.

**Shot 5** (title hold, 1400 ms) renders the `'SQUID GAME'` ALL-CAPS title plate via the `titlePlate` shot kind (the primitive's `'palette-jump-cut'` bundle dispatches `titlePlate` shots through `renderTitlePlate` at `font.size * 2` per `title-sequence.tsx:389`). The container background is still teal (the most-recent `colorPanel` was shot 4 at 2400 ms, color `#067162`); the title text renders at `foreground: #FFFFFF` on top.

If the Implementer's render shows the title plate's font is too small at `font.size * 2` (≈ 192 px against a 720 px canvas height), bump `font.size` from the bundle default (96) to `120` or `140` via the `font` prop override in `buildProps`. Document the choice. Do NOT alter the shot composition.

### D-T350-4. ○△□ symbols — inline as Unicode glyphs, no new shot kind

Two options were considered:

- **(a) Inline as Unicode glyphs** in `colorPanel.content.glyph` (`'○'`, `'△'`, `'□'`); use the existing `colorPanel` render path (primitive lines 507–527 render `glyph` at `font.size * 1.5` centered in `foreground` color). Single render path; zero carve-out cost; no font-registry pressure (the symbols are single Unicode code points U+25CB / U+25B3 / U+25A1 rendered by the system stack or any geometric sans-serif fallback).
- **(b) Document as out-of-scope** for the preset; ship without symbols; carve out `T-350a` for a dedicated symbol-track shot kind.

**Decision: (a)**. The symbols are single Unicode glyphs already rendered by the system stack (the primitive's font fallback chain is `Inter Display, Anton, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif` per `title-sequence.tsx:167–172` — every fallback in that chain renders the geometric Unicode block at U+25A0–U+25FF). No bespoke font; no carve-out cost; the `colorPanel.content.glyph` schema field is bounded `1..3` characters (T-321 D-T321-4 line 136) so single-char glyphs fit cleanly.

If the rendered glyph appearance diverges materially from the canonical drama logo (e.g., the system font's circle reads as a thin outline rather than a filled disk; the triangle appears too narrow), document the divergence in the PR body and proceed — the canonical drama uses bespoke artwork, not a Unicode glyph, and the OFL fallback chain ships text-only. If the Reviewer demands an artwork-faithful symbol track, escalate per CLAUDE.md §6; that is the carve-out path (`T-350a`), not a T-350 fix.

### D-T350-5. Reference frame for the parity golden — frame 105 (= 3500 ms @ 30 fps; mid shot 5; full title visible on teal)

Frame **105** (= `frame * 1000 / fps = 105 * 1000 / 30 = 3500` ms; mid-shot-5 at 3600 ms − 100 ms; (3500 − 3600) sits at -100 ms into shot 5's window — wait, shot 5 runs `[3600, 5000)`, so ms 3500 is actually still in shot 4, not shot 5).

Reconciliation: at currentTimeMs = 3500, the active shot is shot 4 (`panel-teal-title`, `[2400, 3600)`) — a teal panel with no glyph. That is **NOT** the golden we want. We want frame **120** (= 4000 ms; mid-shot-5; teal panel + `'SQUID GAME'` title fully assembled). At ms 4000:

- `currentTimeMs ∈ [3600, 5000)` → active shot index = 5 (`title-hold`, `titlePlate` kind).
- The most-recent `colorPanel` up to ms 4000 is shot 4 (color `#067162` = teal); container background bleeds teal.
- `renderTitlePlate` dispatches via `style: 'palette-jump-cut'` (primitive lines 591–602): `shot.kind === 'titlePlate'` → `body = renderTitlePlate(input)`. Renders `'SQUID GAME'` at `font.size * 2` in `foreground: #FFFFFF` (or `highlightColor` if set; per D-T350-7 we leave `highlightColor` unset, so foreground white wins).
- Per-shot entrance: `entrance: 'fade'` (default) over 12 frames starting at shot 5's start frame (3600 ms ÷ 1000 × 30 = 108). At frame 120 (= 4000 ms), entrance has settled (frame 108 + 12 = 120 — the entrance window closes EXACTLY at frame 120, opacity = 1). Acceptable boundary; if floating-point drift produces opacity 0.999 instead of 1.0, the parity golden still reads as a fully-opaque title.

**Canonical reference frame: 120** (= 4000 ms). Stub line 50 lists candidate frames "0 (pre-entry), 30 (first symbol), 60 (full title), 120 (steady)" — frame 120 (the "steady" reference) is one of those four; pick it.

If the entrance-window-closes-exactly-at-120 boundary produces a partially-opaque title at frame 120 (e.g., opacity 0.95 due to interpolation rounding), shift the canonical frame to **126** (= 4200 ms; well past entrance, well before shot end at 5000 ms). Document the choice in the manifest. Do NOT shift before shot 5's start.

### D-T350-6. Resolver entry shape — clipKind-default `titleSequence → titleSequence`; first Cluster D entry
