# T-364 — Preset: `tiktok-rounded-box` (caption, Cluster F)

**Branch**: `task/T-364-tiktok-rounded-box`

## Goal

Promote `tiktok-rounded-box` from `status: stub` to `status: substantive` and ship it as the **third Cluster F (creator captions) preset** AND the **first Cluster F preset to render with `backdrop: 'pill'`**. The preset captures TikTok's signature auto-caption register: a rounded pill backdrop per word, modest font weight (NOT heavy stroke like Hormozi/MrBeast), high-contrast white foreground on near-opaque dark pills, slide-from-bottom entrance per word, mobile-vertical-friendly. Common uses: short-form vertical voiceover (TikTok / Reels / Shorts cross-posts), viral content, voiceover-driven storytelling, talking-head clips where the pill provides contrast over arbitrary footage.

T-364 is the **third `caption`-clipKind preset**. T-362 took the clipKind-default `caption → caption` slot in `DEFAULT_CLIP_KIND_RESOLVER`; T-363 added the per-`presetId` override path via `PRESET_ID_BINDINGS['mrbeast-komika-axis']`. T-364 follows T-363's mechanism — `PRESET_ID_BINDINGS['tiktok-rounded-box']` — because the `'tiktok'` STYLE_BUNDLE differs from T-362's `'hormozi'` bundle along multiple axes (`backdrop: 'pill'` vs `'none'`; `casing: 'as-is'` vs `'uppercase'`; `strokeWidth: 0` vs `6`; `entrance: 'slide-from-bottom'` vs `'rise'`) and cannot share T-362's `captionBinding`.

T-364 is **M-sized** — same envelope as T-362 / T-363. The CaptionClip primitive (T-316) is on `main`; the `'tiktok'` STYLE_BUNDLE + `backdrop: 'pill'` per-word rendering path + `'slide-from-bottom'` entrance branch are all already shipped (verified during spec drafting — D-T364-13 / D-T364-14). Two new surfaces validated together: per-word pill backdrop rendering + slide-from-bottom entrance.

### D-T364-5. Canonical caption text — `"Wait until you see this"`

Five words, plausible TikTok register (anticipation-beat / hook), conversational mid-pace. Five-word length:
- Single line at size 72 across `width: 1536` (system-stack 700 ≈ 80 px wide per char × 5 short words + spaces fits comfortably).
- Catches the slide entrance + pill backdrop + active word visibly with siblings.
- Distinguishes from T-362 (`"This will change your life forever"`, six words, declarative) and T-363 (`"I gave away one million dollars"`, six words, contest).

Cluster owners rebrand at compose time; preset contract is **shape** (per-word pill + sentence-case + slide entrance + white-on-dark), not the literal text. Alternative considered: `"This is your sign to start"` (six words, similar register; either works). Prefer the five-word phrase to keep the parity-golden line-width unambiguously single-line.

### D-T364-6. WordTiming[] shape — 400ms per word; canonical mid-hold at frame 45 (= 1500ms)

Five words × **400 ms** = **2000 ms** total. TikTok captions are mid-pace (slower than Hormozi at 300 ms; faster than MrBeast at 350 ms emphasis-heavy). Mid-hold = **`frame: 45`** (= `1500 ms` @ 30 fps).

At frame 45:
- Words 1–4 (`Wait` / `until` / `you` / `see`) past their `startMs` and past their `endMs` → all visible, all in **rest** state (not active).
- Word 4 (`see`, `endMs: 1600`) is just past active (1500 ≥ 1200 AND 1500 < 1600 → active). Word 5 (`this`) starts at 1600 — still pre-entrance at frame 45.

Wait — recompute. At `currentTimeMs = 1500`:
- Word 1 (0..400): visible, NOT active
- Word 2 (400..800): visible, NOT active
- Word 3 (800..1200): visible, NOT active
- Word 4 (1200..1600): visible, **active** (`1200 <= 1500 < 1600`)
- Word 5 (1600..2000): NOT yet visible (`currentTimeMs < startMs`)

Visible words: **four**. Active: word 4 (`see`). Word 5 not yet entered. The slide-from-bottom entrance for word 5 begins at `(1600 - 4*80)/1000 * 30 = 38.4` frame (entrance window 12 frames → settles ~50.4) — at frame 45 word 5 is **mid-entrance** (sliding up, ~55% opacity, ~18 px below final position).

Net visual: four pill-backed words settled with active-word equal to word 4 (`see`) + word 5 (`this`) mid-slide entering — crisp evidence of per-word pills + active-word state + slide-from-bottom mid-flight in a single frame.

If a tighter single-frame "all five settled" reference is preferred, shift to **`frame: 60`** (= `2000 ms`): all five visible, word 5 (`this`) just landed active (boundary-equal at 2000, depending on strict-less-than rule) or just-past-active (in rest). Both frame choices are acceptable; **prefer frame 45** because it shows the slide entrance in motion (the preset's named feature) — not a fully-settled state. Document the choice in the manifest. Do NOT introduce a second variant.

If floating-point drift makes word 4 just-past-active or word 3 active instead, acceptable — the parity contract is "four pills visible + one slide mid-flight + sentence-case white-on-dark." Document whichever word lands active.

### D-T364-7. Font posture — `SYSTEM_FONT_STACK` rendered (bundle ships system stack); preferredFont declared as Inter OFL

The `'tiktok'` STYLE_BUNDLE ships `font.family: SYSTEM_FONT_STACK` (NOT Inter or TikTok Sans). Stub declares `preferredFont.family: TikTok Sans` (license `platform-byo`) and `fallbackFont: Source Sans 3` weight 600 (license `ofl`).

**Reconcile**: TikTok Sans is BYO (TikTok-platform-only license, not redistributable); Source Sans 3 is the OFL fallback declared in the stub. The bundle's `SYSTEM_FONT_STACK` (San Francisco / Segoe UI / Roboto / Helvetica) renders in puppeteer's headless Chromium as the platform-default sans (typically Helvetica or Liberation Sans). For v1 parity, this is **acceptable** — the visual register (sentence-case + pill backdrop + white foreground + slide entrance) reads independently of the precise glyph set.

**Frontmatter delta**: keep `preferredFont: { family: TikTok Sans, license: platform-byo }`; keep `fallbackFont: { family: Source Sans 3, weight: 600, license: ofl }`. **Optionally** revise weight to `700` to match the bundle's `weight: 700` (not 600) — both are defensible (Source Sans 3 ships weights 200–900; the TikTok register at size 72 reads natively at 600–700; the bundle ships 700). Prefer **700** for tighter alignment with the bundle's render output.

If a Reviewer demands the bundle's `font.family` field be overridden in `buildProps` to `'Inter, ${SYSTEM_FONT_STACK}'` (forcing Inter when available), the Implementer may do so AND document. Default: do NOT override; let the bundle render. Inter / Source Sans 3 / TikTok Sans are NOT in the FontManager preload list (T-362-flagged primitive-side follow-up).

Verify `Source Sans 3` (OFL, Adobe via Google Fonts) is in `THIRD_PARTY.md`'s OFL block. Add a one-line note if absent. `TikTok Sans` is BYO — no `THIRD_PARTY.md` entry required.

### D-T364-11. Background — mid-tone neutral `#5A5A5A` (gray) for the parity golden; pills provide local contrast

T-362 used `#0E0E12` near-black; T-363 inherited the canvas-bleed quirk. T-364 differs: TikTok captions are designed to overlay arbitrary video — the pill backdrop IS the local contrast layer. Rendering the parity golden on a near-black background hides the pill (black-on-black). Render on a **mid-tone neutral `#5A5A5A`** (gray, 35% lightness) so:
- Pills (`#000000` @ 0.9 opacity) read as dark rounded rects against the gray.
- White text reads cleanly on the pill.
- The slide-from-bottom mid-flight word reads against the gray (no stroke means no contrast halo on the unfilled slide path).

Per T-362 D-T362-11: `CaptionClip` container is `backgroundColor: 'transparent'`; canvas bleeds. The bundle's `background: '#000000'` does NOT reach the canvas (only fills the per-word pill). The canvas color is set by the composition. T-364 inherits — but with backdrop pills, the canvas-bleed quirk is **less visible** because pills supply local contrast. The composition's canvas-background should be set to `#5A5A5A` for T-364's golden. If the prod renderer's canvas defaults to white and the host generator doesn't expose a canvas-background flag, the white bleeds through and the pills still read (black-on-white) — acceptable v1 fallback. Document the choice in the manifest.

If the generator exposes `--canvas-background` or composition-level background, pass `#5A5A5A`. If not, accept the white bleed and document.

### D-T364-12. Position — center-screen `{ x: 192, y: 540, width: 1536, alignment: 'center' }`

TikTok captions in vertical-mobile content typically sit center-screen or slightly upper-center (avoiding the bottom 20–25% reserved for TikTok platform UI). The parity-golden composition is 1920×1080 landscape (cluster convention) — so "center-screen" in the parity world equals the canvas center. 1920×1080 canvas, 10% horizontal inset, vertical center, 80% width, center-aligned. Differentiates from T-362 (lower-third `y: 648`) and matches T-363 (`y: 540`) in the parity-golden corpus.

Body §Rules documents the vertical-mobile origin (1080×1920 at runtime; bottom 20% reserved for platform UI; default vertical position is center / upper-center). The parity contract is the visual register, not the canvas aspect.

### D-T364-13. **Per-word pill backdrop — VERIFIED in T-316**

### D-T364-14. **`slide-from-bottom` entrance — VERIFIED in T-316**
