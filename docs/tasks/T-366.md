# T-366 — Preset: `netflix-invisible` (caption, strict accessibility, Cluster F)

**Branch**: `task/T-366-netflix-invisible`

## Goal

Promote `netflix-invisible` from `status: stub` to `status: substantive` and ship it as the **fifth Cluster F (creator captions) preset**. The preset captures Netflix's strict-accessibility long-form-video subtitle register — the most austere of the cluster. The signature contract is a **near-total active-only visibility model**: only the active word renders at full opacity; past and future words are completely invisible (`muteOpacity: 0`). The strictest active-word emphasis in the cluster F register space, paired with a translucent black backdrop rectangle (`backdrop: 'rect'`, opacity 0.7), small 1 px black stroke for canvas-bleed contrast, off-white text, and `entrance: 'none'`. Common uses: long-form-video accessibility captions, hard-of-hearing-audience subtitles, deaf-audience compliance per FCC / Netflix style-guide rules.

T-366 is the **fifth `caption`-clipKind preset**. Like T-363 / T-364 / T-365 it follows the per-`presetId` resolver-override path (`PRESET_ID_BINDINGS['netflix-invisible']`) because the `'netflix'` STYLE_BUNDLE differs from T-362's `'hormozi'` along every axis (`font` Netflix Sans + Inter fallback vs Montserrat; weight 500 vs 900; size 56 vs 96; `casing: 'as-is'` vs `'uppercase'`; **`muteOpacity: 0`** vs `1`; `strokeWidth: 1` vs `6`; `backdrop: 'rect'` opacity `0.7` vs `'none'`; `entrance: 'none'` vs `'rise'`; `staggerMs: 0` vs `80`) and cannot share `captionBinding`.

T-366 is **M-sized** — same envelope as T-362 / T-363 / T-364 / T-365. CaptionClip primitive (T-316) is on `main`; the `'netflix'` STYLE_BUNDLE + the `muteOpacity: 0` past/future-invisible routing path (T-316a's just-merged fix) + the `backdrop: 'rect'` translucent-rectangle render path + `entrance: 'none'` short-circuit are all already shipped. One new surface validated: the **strictest active-only visibility register** in the Cluster F suite — the only Cluster F preset where past/future visible words drop to zero opacity, plus the only Cluster F preset shipping a non-`'none'` `backdrop` value other than T-364's `'pill'`.

### D-T366-5. Canonical caption text — `"Captions enable accessibility for everyone"`

Five words, plausible accessibility-PSA register (thematic alignment with Netflix's strict-accessibility ethos). Five-word length:
- Single line at size 56 across `width: 1024` (Netflix Sans / Inter 500 ≈ 28 px wide per char × five short / medium words + spaces; "accessibility" is 13 chars and is the longest).
- Catches the active-only visibility contract (one active word visible at opacity 1.0; all other words at opacity 0 — visually absent).
- Distinguishes from T-362 (`"This will change your life forever"`, six words) / T-363 (`"I gave away one million dollars"`, six words) / T-364 (`"Wait until you see this"`, five words) / T-365 (`"The best way to learn is by teaching"`, eight words).
- Sentence case (`'as-is'` casing) — first-letter capitalization only on `"Captions"`. Matches Netflix's mixed-case typography rule.

Cluster owners rebrand at compose time; preset contract is **shape** (sentence-case + active-only visibility + 1 px stroke + translucent rect backdrop + no entrance), not the literal text.

### D-T366-6. WordTiming[] shape — 400ms per word; canonical mid-hold at frame 45 (= 1500ms)

Five words × **400 ms** = **2000 ms** total. Netflix register is slow / readable (subtitles are infrastructure; readable for hard-of-hearing audiences). Mid-hold = **`frame: 45`** (= `1500 ms` @ 30 fps).

At `currentTimeMs = 1500`:
- Word 1 (0..400): visible per-routing but `muteOpacity: 0` → INVISIBLE (past)
- Word 2 (400..800): visible per-routing but `muteOpacity: 0` → INVISIBLE (past)
- Word 3 (800..1200): visible per-routing but `muteOpacity: 0` → INVISIBLE (past)
- Word 4 (1200..1600): visible AND **active** (`1200 <= 1500 < 1600`) → opacity 1.0 (the only visible word)
- Word 5 (1600..2000): NOT yet visible (`currentTimeMs < startMs`)

Visible words (rendered at non-zero opacity): **exactly one** — word 4 (`'for'`). Words 1, 2, 3 are routed but render at opacity 0 (effectively invisible). Word 5 has not entered. Translucent black rectangle backdrop renders behind word 4's region.

Net visual: a single Netflix Sans / Inter weight-500 white sentence-case word `for` (only `'C'` of "Captions" was sentence-case-capitalized, but it's now invisible) on a translucent-black-rect backdrop, with a 1 px black stroke around the visible glyph for canvas-bleed contrast. The rest of the canvas is whatever the parity-fixture canvas defaults to (likely white-or-grey; documented per D-T366-11). This single-word visible state IS the strict-accessibility "active word only" contract; the parity golden's stylistic point is the **opacity-zero past-words** (no faint ghost of past words) plus the **translucent rect backdrop** + the **1 px stroke**.

If floating-point drift makes word 3 (`'accessibility'`) just-past-active or word 4 boundary-edge, acceptable — the parity contract is "exactly one Netflix-styled word visible mid-phrase, translucent rect backdrop behind it, 1 px black stroke, NO ghost of past words." Document whichever word lands active.

Alternative considered: `frame: 60` (= 2000 ms) — word 5 active or just-past. Picking `frame: 45` lands word 4 (`'for'`) active because `'for'` is shorter than `'accessibility'` and visually demonstrates the backdrop-rect width tracking the active-word region. Either would work; **prefer frame 45**. Do NOT introduce a second variant.

### D-T366-11. Background — black-bundle-on-canvas posture (T-362 / T-363 symmetric)

Per T-362 D-T362-11: `CaptionClip` container is `backgroundColor: 'transparent'`; canvas bleeds. The bundle's `background: '#000000'` is declared but only honored when `backdrop !== 'none'` is full-line; the bundle ships `backdrop: 'rect'` which renders a translucent black rectangle ONLY behind the active word's region — NOT a full-canvas background.

**Impact for T-366** is the same as T-362 / T-363 (white text on black bundle but rendered against whatever the canvas defaults to). Unlike T-365's reversed polarity (where the white canvas was on-brand for dark-on-light), T-366's bundle assumes a **dark canvas** (Netflix's player chrome is black) — but the parity-fixture canvas defaults to white (or whatever the host generator emits). The translucent rect backdrop (`opacity 0.7`) provides local contrast for the active word; the canvas-bleed quirk reduces visual fidelity vs the intended Netflix-player register but does NOT break the visual-contract identifiability (the active word + its translucent-rect backdrop is still the primary visible artifact).

`buildProps` does NOT pass an explicit `background` override (the bundle's `'#000000'` is respected even though it never reaches the canvas — declared for documentation alignment with the visual register). If contrast becomes too low (e.g., a near-white canvas + translucent-rect-on-white reads washed-out), the Implementer may pass an explicit `background: '#000000'` in `buildProps` AND document — but note: the bundle's `backdrop: 'rect'` means even an explicit `background` prop won't render a full-canvas backdrop. The composition-level canvas background is the only knob, and the parity-fixture renderer's canvas-default is the constraint.

### D-T366-13. **Strictest opacity-emphasis register — `muteOpacity: 0` VERIFIED in T-316 + T-316a routing**

**Verified during spec drafting** by reading `caption.tsx`:
- `STYLE_BUNDLES['netflix']` (lines 194–208): `highlightColor: '#FFFFFF'`, `muteColor: '#FFFFFF'`, `muteOpacity: 0`, `foreground: '#FFFFFF'`. All three colors are equal (`#FFFFFF` pure white). The only delta between active and non-active visible words is **opacity** (`1.0` for active, **`0.0`** for muted past/future words — i.e., past/future words are completely invisible).
