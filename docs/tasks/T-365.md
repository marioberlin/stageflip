# T-365 — Preset: `ali-abdaal-opacity-karaoke` (caption, Cluster F)

**Branch**: `task/T-365-ali-abdaal-opacity-karaoke`

## Goal

Promote `ali-abdaal-opacity-karaoke` from `status: stub` to `status: substantive` and ship it as the **fourth Cluster F (creator captions) preset**. The preset captures Ali Abdaal's clean-aesthetic education-creator caption register — the **opposite-of-Hormozi**: Inter 600 sentence-case dark gray on white, **no stroke**, **no backdrop**, **no per-word entrance**, and an **opacity-only active-word emphasis** (active word at full opacity, past/future words muted to 0.6). Common uses: education / lecture-style YouTube long-form, productivity-creator vlogs, podcast-clip captions, talking-head explainers — any "professional reading aesthetic" register.

T-365 is the **fourth `caption`-clipKind preset**. Like T-363 / T-364 it follows the per-`presetId` resolver-override path (`PRESET_ID_BINDINGS['ali-abdaal-opacity-karaoke']`) because the `'ali-abdaal'` STYLE_BUNDLE differs from T-362's `'hormozi'` along every axis (`font` Inter vs Montserrat; `casing: 'as-is'` vs `'uppercase'`; `foreground: '#1F1F1F'` on white vs white on dark; `muteOpacity: 0.6` opacity-based emphasis vs no-mute; `strokeWidth: 0` vs `6`; `entrance: 'none'` vs `'rise'`; `staggerMs: 0` vs `80`) and cannot share `captionBinding`.

T-365 is **M-sized** — same envelope as T-362 / T-363 / T-364. CaptionClip primitive (T-316) is on `main`; the `'ali-abdaal'` STYLE_BUNDLE + opacity-based active-word emphasis (`muteColor === foreground` + `muteOpacity: 0.6`) + `entrance: 'none'` short-circuit are all already shipped (verified during spec drafting — D-T365-13 / D-T365-14). One new surface validated: the opacity-only emphasis path with NO color shift, NO stroke, NO backdrop, NO entrance — the minimum-decoration end of the Cluster F register space.

### D-T365-5. Canonical caption text — `"The best way to learn is by teaching"`

Eight words, plausible Ali register (classic education-creator aphorism — productivity / learning-aesthetic; near-attribution of "Feynman technique" wisdom without a literal quote). Eight-word length:
- Single line at size 64 across `width: 1024` (Inter 600 ≈ 35–40 px wide per char × short words + spaces fits ~one line; may flex-wrap to two short lines depending on rendered metrics — both acceptable).
- Catches the opacity-emphasis (one active word visible at full opacity + seven visible-but-muted siblings) clearly.
- Distinguishes from T-362 (`"This will change your life forever"`, six words) / T-363 (`"I gave away one million dollars"`, six words) / T-364 (`"Wait until you see this"`, five words).
- Sentence case (`'as-is'` casing) — first-letter capitalization only on `"The"`. Crucial: Ali register depends on lowercase reading; do NOT normalize to title-case.

Cluster owners rebrand at compose time; preset contract is **shape** (sentence-case + opacity-only emphasis + no stroke / backdrop / entrance), not the literal text.

### D-T365-6. WordTiming[] shape — 300ms per word; canonical mid-hold at frame 60 (= 2000ms)

Eight words × **300 ms** = **2400 ms** total. Ali Abdaal register is mid-pace (slower than Hormozi's mid-pace 300 ms feel because no entrance-stagger overlaps; words read at their declared pace). Mid-hold = **`frame: 60`** (= `2000 ms` @ 30 fps).

At `currentTimeMs = 2000`:
- Word 1 (0..300): visible, NOT active (past)
- Word 2 (300..600): visible, NOT active (past)
- Word 3 (600..900): visible, NOT active (past)
- Word 4 (900..1200): visible, NOT active (past)
- Word 5 (1200..1500): visible, NOT active (past)
- Word 6 (1500..1800): visible, NOT active (past)
- Word 7 (1800..2100): visible, **active** (`1800 <= 2000 < 2100`)
- Word 8 (2100..2400): NOT yet visible (`currentTimeMs < startMs`)

Visible words: **seven**. Active: word 7 (`'by'`). Six past words at opacity 0.6; word 7 at opacity 1.0; word 8 not yet entered (`entrance: 'none'` means it pops in at frame 63 with no transition).

Net visual: seven sentence-case dark-gray words on white canvas, six muted (opacity 0.6) and one fully-opaque (the seventh, `'by'`); no stroke; no backdrop; no entrance animation. The single delta between active and rest IS opacity — crisp evidence of the opacity-karaoke contract in one frame.

If floating-point drift makes word 6 (`'is'`) just-past-active or word 7 boundary-edge, acceptable — the parity contract is "seven visible sentence-case dark-gray words on white, exactly one at full opacity, the rest at ~0.6 opacity." Document whichever word lands active.

Alternative considered: `frame: 75` (= 2500 ms) — all eight words visible, word 8 active. Either would work; **prefer frame 60** because it visibly demonstrates the past-words-mute pattern (six muted past words is more emphatic than seven). Do NOT introduce a second variant.

### D-T365-7. Font posture — Inter OFL (rendered); preferredFont declared as TT Fors commercial-byo

The `'ali-abdaal'` STYLE_BUNDLE ships `font.family: 'Inter, ${SYSTEM_FONT_STACK}'` (Inter primary; SF / Segoe / Roboto / Helvetica fallback). Stub declares `preferredFont.family: TT Fors` (license `commercial-byo`) and `fallbackFont: Poppins` weight 700 (license `ofl`).

**Reconcile**: TT Fors is a commercial display sans (TypeType foundry) — BYO. Poppins (Google Fonts SIL OFL 1.1) is the declared OFL fallback. The bundle, however, ships **Inter** (OFL, Google Fonts; weight 600) — NOT Poppins. In headless puppeteer, Inter is loadable from the host font registry if registered; otherwise the system-stack fallback renders.

**Frontmatter delta**: keep `preferredFont: { family: TT Fors, license: commercial-byo }`. **Revise** `fallbackFont` to **`{ family: Inter, weight: 600, license: ofl }`** to match the bundle's actual `font.family` (Inter at weight 600). Inter and Poppins are both geometric sans at similar metrics; the bundle ships Inter. Reconciling the declared fallback to match the implementation is the correct posture (T-364 D-T364-7 precedent — declared fallback aligns with what the bundle actually renders).

If a Reviewer prefers leaving Poppins as the declared fallback (on the grounds that Poppins is a closer metric match to TT Fors than Inter is), the Implementer may keep `Poppins weight 700` and instead document the bundle's Inter rendering as a known T-316 follow-up. Default: **switch to Inter 600** for tighter alignment.

Verify `Inter` (OFL, Google Fonts via Rasmus Andersson) is in `THIRD_PARTY.md`'s OFL block — likely already present from prior tasks; add a one-line note if absent. `TT Fors` is BYO — no `THIRD_PARTY.md` entry required.

### D-T365-13. **Opacity-based active-word emphasis — VERIFIED in T-316**

**Verified during spec drafting** by reading `caption.tsx`:

### D-T365-14. **`entrance: 'none'` short-circuit — VERIFIED in T-316**

**Verified during spec drafting** by reading `caption.tsx` line 191 (`entrance: 'none'`) + the entrance routing branch. `entrance: 'none'` short-circuits the `'rise'` / `'bounce'` / `'slide-from-bottom'` interpolation: words appear at their settled position with no per-word translate / scale / opacity interpolation from frame `(startMs - staggerMs * i) / 1000 * fps`. Words pop in at their `startMs` boundary at full opacity (then immediately drop to 0.6 in the next frame if past).

Stub prose says "entire phrase appears at once on the panel (panel scales 0.97 → 1.0, 250 ms)" — the bundle does NOT implement panel-scale entrance (no panel rendered per `backdrop: 'none'`). Body §Animation revises stub prose to match the implemented bundle (no entrance animation at all; words pop in at their `startMs` boundary). Document delta in PR body.

For T-365's snapshot (eight words × 300 ms, `entrance: 'none'`, `staggerMs: 0`), at frame 60 (= 2000 ms) words 1–7 are all already visible (no entrance timing) and word 8 is below its `startMs` (2100 ms) so not yet rendered. Frame 60 captures the steady mid-phrase opacity-karaoke state.

If Implementer's reading contradicts (e.g., `entrance: 'fade'` instead of `'none'`, or there's hidden phrase-level scaling), escalate. If the Implementer prefers a fully-settled all-eight-visible reference frame, shift to `frame: 75` per D-T365-6 fallback. Both frames are defensible; document the choice.
