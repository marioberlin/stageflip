# T-335 — Preset: `nbc-snf-possession-illuminated` (scoreBug, Cluster B)

**Branch**: `task/T-335-nbc-snf-possession`

## Goal

Promote the existing `nbc-snf-possession-illuminated` preset stub from `status: stub` to `status: substantive` and ship it as the **third Cluster B (sports / score-bug) preset to land** AND the **fourth consumer of the `scoreBug` clipKind** in the parity-CLI resolver AND the **third production consumer of T-332a's `'football'` style bundle** (after T-333 PL and T-334 Fox NFL) AND the **first production consumer of three additional T-332a primitive features**: the `centerCircle: boolean` prop (renders a black circle above the clock — used here to host the NBC logo glyph), the `direction: 'left-to-right' | 'right-to-left'` prop (encodes play direction for chevron-prefixed down-and-distance), and the `networkLogo: string` prop (the glyph rendered inside the center circle). The preset captures NBC's Sunday Night Football register: a horizontal bar bottom-center on a dark `#0A0A0A` base, NFC vs AFC team flanks (KC red `#E31837` home + BUF navy `#00338D` away — Sunday Night canonical AFC matchup), white text, a center treatment that nests game clock + period + a black circle hosting the NBC mark, possession illumination on the home team box (KC has the ball; primitive applies `filter: brightness(1.12)`), directional chevrons on down-and-distance (`'<< 1st & 10'` — left-to-right play direction, the compass-flagged "genuine innovation"), and Public Sans 600 (SemiBold) as the OFL fallback for the proprietary Sweet Sans Pro + NBC Tinker pairing. Common uses: live NFL match overlays where the bottom-center horizontal bar + dark base + center NBC circle + directional chevrons register is the brand signal — sister-but-distinct from T-333's PL upper-left rectangular chrome AND T-334's Fox NFL chromeless gradient.

T-335 is the **fourth** preset to bind the `scoreBug` clipKind. T-358 (`cricket-ball-by-ball-dots`) holds the **clipKind-default** slot (`scoreBug → scoreBugDotsBinding`; binds `outcome-row` primitive, NOT `score-bug` — predates T-332a). T-333 (`premier-league-field-of-play`, merged at 7f990ab) and T-334 (`fox-nfl-no-chrome`, merged at 35d97ff) added the **first two `PRESET_ID_BINDINGS` overrides** keyed on `'premier-league-field-of-play'` / `'fox-nfl-no-chrome'`, both binding the `score-bug` primitive's `'football'` style branch. T-335 wires its binding via a **third `PRESET_ID_BINDINGS` override** keyed on `'nbc-snf-possession-illuminated'` — Pattern C from `docs/handover-phase13-mid.md` §2 (n-th-preset-for-clipKind via `PRESET_ID_BINDINGS`). Mirrors T-334's resolver-binding shape verbatim, swapping `fox-nfl-no-chrome → nbc-snf-possession-illuminated` AND substituting NBC SNF-specific snapshot constants. T-358's clipKind-default arm is **NOT** touched. T-333's `premierLeagueFopBinding` and T-334's `foxNflNoChromeBinding` + their respective `PRESET_ID_BINDINGS` entries are **NOT** touched.

T-335 is the **third production consumer of T-332a's `'football'` style branch** (T-333 was first; T-334 was second). It is also the **first production consumer** of three optional fields on the `'football'` schema that neither T-333 nor T-334 exercised: `centerCircle`, `direction`, and `networkLogo`. T-333 exercises only required football fields + casing/font; T-334 first-consumed `backdropGradient` + `down` + `possession`; T-335 first-consumes `centerCircle` + `direction` + `networkLogo` AND second-consumes `down` + `possession` (after T-334). Together, T-333 / T-334 / T-335 exercise every optional field on the `'football'` schema except `accent` (no current Cluster B consumer requires the accent slot).

T-335 is **M-sized** — same envelope as T-334 / T-333. ~30–45 LOC binding + snapshot constant + single steady-state parity golden + sign-off in-PR. ~3 LOC over T-334's envelope to carry `centerCircle` + `direction` + `networkLogo` in the snapshot constant (one-line scalar each; no nested object). No primitive carve-out; no schema change. Inherits T-334's resolver shape end-to-end (`PRESET_ID_BINDINGS` override path; clipKind fall-through unchanged).
